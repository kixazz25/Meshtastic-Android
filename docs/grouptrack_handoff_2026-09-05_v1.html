The three documents — where they are and what they are

User Manual — grouptrack_manual_2026-09-05.html (7 MB, 112 figures)

Rebuilt from scratch today, modelled on the old manual's voice: numbered steps, second person, guidance rather than description.

Structure, settled after three rounds with Fred:

  • Opens as four collapsed lines — About GroupTrack · Meshtastic · Ride Planning · Ride Map. ⭐ In the order things happen: set up the radio, plan the ride, then ride it.
  • Five levels of nesting, each indented further with its own colour. Planning → Map Features → Route+ → AI Assisted → the six steps.
  • Accordion — opening a section closes its siblings, never an ancestor. The path you came down stays open.
  • ⭐ A section with an open child hides its own figures and prose. Fred: "screens at each level and six at the end is unnavigatable." You keep the trail of headings; only the level you are on shows its screens.
  • Expand all / collapse all in a sticky bar.

⛔ SHARED CONTROLS ARE DUPLICATED, DELIBERATELY. Fred: "you cannot say A-F are covered somewhere else. I don't want to have to exist somewhere else to look things up." The planner carries its own Back to Ride, SAT/TOPO/TOPO+, QUEUES and Search with their own figures, and the four popups appear on both surfaces.

⚠ This is the opposite of the code rule, and on purpose. The cost is drift — change how Search works and two sections need updating. Accepted, because the alternative is a reader bouncing between sections to answer one question.
