⭐⭐⭐ The finding — the mesh wire format is already in the repo

core/proto/src/main/proto/meshtastic/atak.proto, with TAKPacket.kt already generated. Portnum 72 (ATAK_FORWARDER). Standard Meshtastic — every node relays it, every TAK-aware app reads it.

⭐ So the layer 1 / layer 2 contract is settled and it is not ours to design. TAKPacket in, TAKPacket out; layer 2 cannot tell which transport it came from. That is Fred's parity rule expressed as a published format rather than an interface we would have had to invent.

⛔ But the internal model must NOT become TAKPacket. Parse at the boundary into ConvoyNode, emit at the boundary — or a tactical vocabulary leaks upward.

⭐ And most of "convoy" never goes on the wire. Gaps, distance to lead and tail, off-track — ConvoyEngine computes all of it locally. The payload question was only ever position, identity and role.

⛔ Three things I asserted today that were wrong

⛔ "A stock radio can only carry a Position packet." The radio carries whatever port a connected app pushes through IMeshService. The constraint is on the APP, not the radio.

⛔ "Lead must be local because role cannot travel." Group.role travels natively. ⚠ Local selection is still the better default — no configuration, no election, no conflict when two riders claim it, works before any role is received — but it is now a CHOICE, not a constraint.

⛔ "The CoT bridge is Natak's and they would have to extend it." The Nucleus bridge is theirs; the Meshtastic ATAK path is upstream and in our fork.

⚠ UNVERIFIED — DO NOT BUILD ON IT: that Meshtastic Android 2.8.0+ ships a built-in local TAK server. If true and if our fork is recent enough, we may already hold the merge component. ⛔ Check what we forked from first.

Natak Mesh — vendor answers, same day

Nathan Boyette answered all 24 questions. Full detail in the master under NATAK-0915. The four that matter:

  • ⭐⭐ Nucleus_OS is MIT. No obligations. Clean, against the GPL entanglement we are removing.
  • ⭐ Multicast forwards across the whole mesh — L3 routing, all nodes see all. The broadcast model holds.
  • ⭐ ~25 nodes at 5 s updates, ~80 at 15 s. Ten riders is comfortably inside. "The radios are being pushed" does not hold for this hardware.
  • ⛔ The V3 has NO Bluetooth — everything through the 5.8 GHz AP and meshtasticd. On a Nucleus, even mesh is reached over IP. Layer 1 needs two connection paths (BLE for a bare radio, TCP for a Nucleus) and neither visible above the line.

⚠ Gaps he named himself: no 12 V input (5 V, 0.65 A), no shock provisions (suggests potting), UFW only and internet-facing security not a focus. All three are partnership deliverables rather than objections.

⭐ He is in on partnership — tailoring hardware/software, a Nucleus preconfigured for a GroupTrack club, bundling to SxS manufacturers and software vendors.

Standing lessons from this week

  • ⛔ Ordering and lifecycle changes get a where-used pass on the CALLEES, shown to Fred, before the patch is written. Moving a load into Application.onCreate without doing that cost 09-14.
  • ⛔ Absence is not evidence until the search covers the thing. find /sdcard missed a file we had just cat'd — the shell cannot traverse Android/data. A *.proto grep under app/src/main/ missed atak.proto in core/proto/.
  • ⛔ Droid 2's clock is on Utah time, two hours behind the laptop. Compare against the device's own clock.
  • ⛔ Assert that a count is UNCHANGED rather than guessing what it should be, and never put the searched-for literal in a patch's replacement text. Three scripts failed their own verifiers on correct edits.
  • ⚠ dumpsys location shows what the SYSTEM holds, not what the app can obtain. Only an in-app read answers the second.
