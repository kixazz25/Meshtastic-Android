⚠ DELETE LESSONS WORTH KEEPING

The first cut used ONE DELETE ... BETWEEN per zoom — that bounded the statement COUNT (~18 vs ~50,000) but not the statement SIZE. Measured: SAT z18 = 62 seconds for 10,295 tiles in one statement (~166 tiles/sec); each row carries a 50–100KB blob, so a high-zoom rectangle is gigabytes of page churn. The big area sat 7.5+ minutes inside a single z17 with no progress and no way to cancel — WorkManager logged "Ignored stopWork … is in foreground". Progress AND isStopped are only checked BETWEEN statements.

⚠ TWO DIFFERENT JOBS — do not conflate them. The BIG area (08:13→~09:xx) is the endurance evidence and did eventually complete. The SMALL area (10:03→10:05, ~75s, 40,912 tiles) is where the clean three-store log came from. Delete is correct at both scales; on a large area it runs long with no feedback — which patch N fixes (bands of ~2,000 tiles/statement, plus a real on-disk pre-count replacing a geometry estimate that showed 1,500,000 for an area holding 38,896).

⚠ "0 MB reclaimable" IS REPRODUCIBLE — reported after 38,896 tiles removed AND after 40,912. Those pages went somewhere; most likely WAL holding them until a checkpoint. Patch N logs raw freelist_count/page_size. Settle this BEFORE designing any VACUUM flow on a number that may be meaningless.

1. OSM TRAIL IMPORT. Governed by two Fred constraints that decide everything: imports are a repeatable USER process (app → Method B → pick area → import; no PC, no script, no hand-typed bbox), and no OSM data on GroupTrack infrastructure ever (an AWS endpoint would be distribution and trigger ODbL share-alike). ⭐ Fred's key insight: buildUrl appends f=geojson, so nothing Esri-specific is in the RESPONSE, only in the QUERY STRING. The work is in the fetch, not the pipeline.

⭐ THREE FINDINGS THAT CAME OUT OPPOSITE TO PREDICTION

  • OSM labels are RICHER than Esri's downloaded roads/places layers — Fred, on device. The prediction was thin OSM coverage in remote Utah and a coverage-for-size trade. Got more content AND less storage.
  • OSM trails are COMPLEMENTARY to UGRC, not redundant — "some duplication but mostly coexistence." ⚠ This read changed TWICE before landing: (1) "very few trails render" at low zoom → looked negligible; (2) UGRC's property dump showed OSM carries no comparable attributes → looked useless; (3) both drawn at z15+ over real geography → complementary. Do not re-open from (1) or (2). OSM minor ways only appear from ~z14 in the OpenMapTiles schema — any coverage comparison must be at z15+.
  • SNAP-2 IS ALREADY RENDERER-INDEPENDENT — RouteManager.snap(lat, lon, trails, tracks, 30.0) at ConvoyMapViewerScreen:441 is Kotlin, no Leaflet API. And the redraw path already resolves by database ID, not by viewport (comment at :1240). That is exactly the discipline that makes it vector-tile-safe — already in place, by design.
