GroupTrack 2.6f

2026-08-16 · Google Play compliance release · branch test/authority-api36 → feature/convoy-event-ride
DRAFT — do not publish until the AAB is built from the merged branch and the version code is confirmed.

2.6f is a compliance and data-safety release. There are no new features. It brings GroupTrack onto Android 16 (API 36) ahead of Google Play's 31 August requirement, rebuilds how the app asks for storage access, and fixes a serious problem in which reinstalling the app could erase your saved trails, tracks and waypoints.

What's fixed

Reinstalling the app no longer erases your data

A one-time database upgrade step could not tell the difference between a brand-new installation and a reinstall over data you already had. On a reinstall it treated your existing databases as out of date and cleared them, so trails, tracks and waypoints were lost while your downloaded maps stayed behind.

This is fixed. The app now records the upgrade alongside your data instead of in storage that an uninstall wipes, and — as a second, independent safeguard — it will not clear a database that still contains records. If it cannot read a database well enough to be sure, it leaves it alone.

Downloaded map tiles were never affected by this and did not need recovering.

A clearer setup screen for storage access

GroupTrack keeps 50–60 GB of offline maps in shared storage so they survive app updates and reinstalls, which is why it needs all-files access. The setup screen now:

  • Only appears when something is actually missing. If access is already granted, GroupTrack goes straight to the map with no prompt.
  • Confirms access by actually using it, rather than trusting the system's answer — which can report access the app does not really have.
  • Tells you plainly what happens if you decline. GroupTrack cannot open your maps, trails or tracks without this access and will not start. Exiting is safe: nothing on your device is changed or removed, and granting access later leaves everything as you left it.
  • No longer gets stuck after you grant access and return to the app.

Background location is now optional

"Allow all the time" location is what lets GroupTrack report your position to your convoy and keep recording a track while the screen is off. You can now skip it and continue into the app.

You will be reminded each time you start GroupTrack while it is turned off — deliberately. Without it, track recording stops when your screen sleeps, and that is not obvious until you get home and find the track is short. You can turn it on at any time in Android settings.

Smaller fixes

  • The Exit button on the setup screen now closes the app. Previously it did nothing.
  • Clearing the app's cache is safe again and no longer resets your setup.

Compatibility

GroupTrack now targets Android 16 (API 36), required by Google Play for app updates from 31 August 2026. Minimum supported version is unchanged at Android 8.0.
