WHAT THE MEASUREMENTS SETTLED

  • ⭐ Land ownership works where eight other approaches failed. Point-in-polygon against SITLA: complaint areas 97% Private, backcountry 74% Federal. The first real separation in ten attempts. It has no coverage hole by construction — every acre is owned by someone.
  • Non-motorized is 70% private — 20,013 of 28,499. 3 - Paved Shared Use alone is 13,493 private against 247 public, so it is sidewalks and park paths. That answered Fred's question about whether a private bucket pays: it does, and most of all there.
  • ⛔ 87% of "Motorized Access" is INFERENCE, not evidence. 28,055 unclassified motorized plus 16,482 bare tracks against ~6,800 actually stated — and all carry motorized_allowed = Y. A farm track in a subdivision reads as rideable on nothing more than highway=track. Splitting stated from inferred is the honest fix.
  • ⭐ charAt(0) constrains the code to ONE CHARACTER, not one DIGIT. All of 0-9 were taken; letters were always free. Neither of us saw it for two days.
  • ⚠ The legend has been wrong all along. trailColor has NEVER had a case for '2', so Hiking and Biking — 7,513 rows — always drew cyan. The 08-30 orange change only made an existing lie collide with something real.
  • ⭐ The audit trail corrected me within minutes of existing. I said step 8 destroyed the identity of 13,628 rows; it was 201 — designated_uses still held the original for the rest. 1.5% of what I claimed.
