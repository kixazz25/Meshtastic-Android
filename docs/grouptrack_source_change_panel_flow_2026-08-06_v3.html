2THE PANEL — explanation, then three options

SAT — set up your maps for Google Hybrid
Your stored tiles came from Esri and cannot be converted. Esri satellite needs two extra label stores for place and road names; Google Hybrid has those names baked into the imagery and does not use them.

Either way you lose labels on your existing Esri tiles — the new source does not fetch label overlays, so those tiles will draw as plain satellite from now on. Clearing at least reclaims the 461 MB the label stores are using.

We recommend clearing for a move from Esri to Google Hybrid — unless you are already running corridor-based tiles, in which case your storage is already efficient and replacing in place keeps your maps working throughout.

If you clear, any reload choice is comfortable. If you are not sure, choose Nothing now: your Esri tiles stay exactly as they are and you can refresh on demand later with Download Tiles by Area or by Track.
1 — EXISTING TILES
Clear themRECOMMENDED Removes 3 stores, frees 4,812 MB. Single source, nothing stale left. Cannot be undone. No backup. Download history is cleared too.
Replace in place NEXT Re-downloads your existing coverage on the new source, keeping your storage method — area stays area, corridor stays corridor. Maps keep working throughout. No space reclaimed. Best if you are already on corridors.
Keep them as they are Nothing removed, nothing downloaded. Your Esri tiles keep working without labels; anything new comes from Google Hybrid.
2 — RELOAD NEXT
All tracks — corridorsIF YOU HAVE TRACKS Corridors around every imported track. Recommended when you have track histories — coverage follows where you actually ride, using about two thirds less storage. 8 tracks · ~161,000 tiles · about 4 hours.
All areasIF YOU DO NOT Rebuilds your area coverage on the new source. Recommended if you downloaded areas and do not intend to import tracks.
Nothing now Nothing is queued. Draw an area or import tracks whenever you like — Download Tiles by Area and by Track are always available.

CANCEL PROCEED

⭐ No picker. All tracks or all areas — whole options, no per-item selection. The explanation does the work a selection UI would have done, and there is far less to build.

⭐ The recommendation is conditional and stated, not computed. Clear unless you are already on corridors · all-tracks if you have tracks · all-areas if you do not · nothing-now if unsure. Four sentences, no decision engine.

⚠ "All areas" needs the area list to survive the clear. Queue entries carry north/south/east/west, but a clear wipes queue history — so clear + all areas cannot both be honoured unless the bboxes are copied into the migration record first. ⭐ Cheap fix: snapshot them into the record at begin().

⛔ CANCEL must complete the record, not just close the panel — otherwise it dangles as a permanently interrupted migration and pollutes the next source change on this column.

3What PROCEED runs

CLEAR HALF  ── BUILT, compiles ───────────────────────────────
1  begin()            record written — ABORT if it cannot be written
2  holdQueue()        no new jobs start
3  cancelAll()        cancels DOWNLOADING workers (keeps COMPLETE)
4  awaitIdle()        poll ≤15s — cancelWorkById is ASYNC and returns early.
                      timeout ⇒ FAIL, do not delete under a live worker
5  clearCompleted()   drains the history cancelAll deliberately kept
6  deleteSource(dir)  per OLD-source cache dir, noteDeleted() after each
7  complete()         + resumeQueue()

RELOAD HALF ── NEXT ──────────────────────────────────────────
8  all tracks  → enqueueCorridorBatch(all hashes, [new slot], replace=false)
   all areas   → area enqueue over snapshotted bboxes, replace=false
   nothing now → no-op, recorded as the choice

⭐ replace=false after a clear, forced and locked: on an empty store there is nothing to replace, and hasTile then deduplicates across the whole batch so overlapping corridors fetch shared tiles once.
