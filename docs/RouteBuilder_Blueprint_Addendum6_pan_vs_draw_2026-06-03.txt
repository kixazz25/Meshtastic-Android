ADDENDUM 2026-06-03 (note for Draw method) — pan vs finger-draw is the SAME gesture

Observed during Stage 1: in Method 1 (tap-to-place) pan works for free because a
tap (click) != a drag. NO conflict, no disarm needed. Confirmed on-device.

BUT for Method 2 (Draw / finger-trace): panning and finger-drawing a trail are the
SAME gesture (press + drag). They collide -- a drag cannot mean both "pan the map"
and "draw the line." This is the core Draw-mode problem.

Resolution (already in blueprint, restated here): the dragIntent axis (PAN vs TRACE).
When Draw is active -> drag = TRACE, pan OFF; an explicit toggle switches drag back
to PAN to reposition, then back to TRACE. THIS is where an arm/disarm-style toggle
finally has a purpose (we deferred adding a toggle until it had one -- Draw is it).

Not a Stage 1 / Method 1 concern. Build with Method 2 (Draw).
