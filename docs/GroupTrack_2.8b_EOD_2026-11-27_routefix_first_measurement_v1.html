Three edits, all in GtRoute.kt, applied 01:00 (backup GtRoute.kt.bak_routefix_20261127_010038).

#changewhy
1Reach-and-return screen moved ahead of the MAXPOI cap. Two origin-only Dijkstra runs over all 30 candidates; unreachable features dropped before the cap sees them. The cap is left in place as a guard and simply never fires. MAXPOI dropped 6 of 30 by score before any distance was measured.

3ranked gets bad-pair count as the tiebreak within a score. scoreSet on a single feature takes two values on this ground, so a 29-member pool sorted into two buckets and hash order decided the rest.

6. Where the duplicates are born

found holds 94 candidates and every one has a distinct feature set. Zero duplicates at the search stage. So the copies are created by enrichment, not by the search.

pre sorts by compareByDescending { it.combo.size }.thenByDescending { it.miles } and takes the top enrichN = 20. Sorting by feature count first means the 20 kept are the most similar candidates in the list -- the ones with the least room to grow differently -- and all 74 low-count rides are discarded unexamined. Each of the 20 is then grown toward 8 features by maximum gain. Greedy growth from similar starting points converges on identical rides: 20 slots, 6 distinct outcomes.

This is the enrichment-convergence defect recorded in the 11-26 EOD doc (then 7 of 20 on the iPad). A better-ordered pool makes it worse: 13 of 20. The improvement in pool quality and the loss of the sixth ride are the same effect.
