Per panel: where riders reach it today (from the click tree), how it is opened, its clickable count, the inputs it asks for, whether it scrolls, the text colours it uses, and its fate in the 2.7c target. Standards (Fred 10-22): every panel scrolls (landscape breaks panels that cannot); one colour scheme for all panels (decided panel by panel in the reviews); details panels separate facts from actions and hide technical data. NO SCROLL = a long panel (over 120 lines or more than 6 buttons) with no scrolling — it breaks in landscape. Inputs and colours are counted from the code of each panel's own function; panels that call helper panels may ask for more.
