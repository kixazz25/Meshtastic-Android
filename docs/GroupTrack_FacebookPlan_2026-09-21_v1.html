How to post

  • Same day each week. Pick one evening before the weekend, when riders are planning, and keep to it — a regular slot is part of being recognisable.
  • Attach the card as the photo, then paste the text. The link stays as text; the card is what people stop for.
  • Answer every comment that day. A developer who replies is the most credible thing in a group.
  • If an admin objects, drop that group rather than argue — seven friendly groups beat eight tense ones.
  • Post 5 (group tracking) has no weekend hook in its text on purpose, so it works whichever week it falls in.

What makes them recognisable

  • The card: GroupTrack wordmark top left, one headline, one real screenshot, and the same footer — Free on Google Play · grouptrack.org.
  • The first line of each post is the card’s headline, word for word, so the picture and the text visibly belong together.
  • Every post ends with the same close: “GroupTrack is free on Google Play. I built it for the way we ride, and I read every comment. — Fred” That line is where “free” lives every week, in one consistent place; each post’s own text only mentions cost where it’s specific to that feature.
  • Every picture is real — a screen from the app or a photo from a ride. No generated images.

4 · Old rides, new trails

Your old rides become new trails.

Export your rides from Gaia or onX as GPX and bring them in. The maps for every ride download on their own, overnight.

Anywhere your tracks cross ground no published map covers, GroupTrack adds that stretch as a new trail. Your rides are proof the ground is rideable. You rode it.
