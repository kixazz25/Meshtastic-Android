⚠ THROUGHPUT NOTE — the overnight slowdown, and what it is NOT

7 PM → 7 AM produced 90 jobs, ~7.5/hour. Daytime was ~24/hour, and the rate roughly doubled again when daytime resumed on 08-09. Overnight ran at about a third of daytime speed.

⭐⭐ DOZE IS THE LEADING EXPLANATION AFTER ALL — AND THE REASON IS THE POWER SOURCE. Droid 2 was charging from the laptop's USB port, and the laptop sleeps. A sleeping host commonly cuts or throttles USB power, so Android may well have seen the device as UNPLUGGED for much of the night — which satisfies all three Doze conditions (unplugged, screen off, stationary). ⚠ "Plugged into a PC" is NOT the same as "on power." Use a wall charger for any overnight run that is being measured.

⚠ RECORD OF THE REASONING, so it is not re-litigated: Doze was proposed → withdrawn when Fred said the device was plugged in → reinstated when he identified the source as a sleeping PC. Neither the first nor the second call was safe on the evidence available at the time.

STILL POSSIBLE ALONGSIDE IT — none ruled out, and all would hit real users:

  • Thermal throttling — leading candidate. Four concurrent slots for twelve hours on charge heats both radio and battery. Check: adb shell dumpsys thermalservice | head -30
  • WiFi power save with the screen off — many devices drop the radio to low power even while charging.
  • OEM background management — vendor-specific, independent of Doze AND of charging.
  • Tile server behaviour — least likely overnight in the US, not impossible.

⚠ THE 08-09 EVENING RUN IS CONFOUNDED — THREE VARIABLES CHANGED AT ONCE: Doze disabled via ADB, battery optimization + all auto-optimization turned off for GroupTrack, and the power situation understood. ⭐ RESULT: 36 tiles/sec — a NEW HIGH, above the 33.2 aggregate measured at 4 slots on 08-08, which had been the ceiling. So the changes did more than recover the daytime rate. ⚠ Which single change delivered it is NOT established.

⭐⭐ PRODUCT CONSEQUENCE, not just a test note: users cannot run ADB. Large corridor downloads left running overnight will hit whatever this is. The app should request a battery optimization exemption (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS), and the manual and quickstart should say plug in AND set the app to Unrestricted before a large download.

⚠ Do not quote overnight completion times from daytime rates until this is settled.
