2. What is NOT a port, and why

  • TileCodec (66) — encodes tiles as WebP through Bitmap.CompressFormat. There is no shared image codec; this is a declared socket per plan §8. G1 defines the seam, each platform implements it. Nothing to certify beyond the seam's contract.
  • ConvoyTileDownloader's fetch (of 346) — OkHttp calls, retry/backoff, worker integration. The inventory already calls the fetch platform. What ports is buildTileUrl and tilePath: string and path math.

⛔ G1g and G1h are the two to be careful with. The 08-08 data-loss incident and the storage conversion are both in this territory: ConvoySourceClear deletes tile store directories, ConvoyCorridorDelete removes tiles by corridor, and GroupTrackConversion moves the rider's whole GroupTrack folder through SAF. Their certs must run on copies and must compare what would be deleted before anything is. They are last in the order for that reason, not because they are least important.
What G1 inherits for free from G2f and G2g: the OLD-copy-with-counted-manifest pattern and its mechanical verification (reverse the manifest, diff against git show 2.7e:…); the fixture-drives-both-sides shape; the Droid/iPad runner-script recipe; and the four harness rules — scripted clock, fixture-ordered listings, sorted collections, and never comparing a file's generated text. GtTileMath and GtStorage already exist in shared.

4. Open question for Fred

G1 is 3,926 lines against G2's 2,488, and G2 took roughly a day and a half. On that arithmetic G1 is two to three days on its own. The piece split above is designed so each piece is independently shippable and switchable — so G1 can be partially complete at any point without leaving the Android app in a broken state. Confirm the split, or reorder it.
