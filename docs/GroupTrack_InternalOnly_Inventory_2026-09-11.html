Then the two-line change

Once every site resolves through the accessor:

  • GroupTrackStorage.root() → ctx.getExternalFilesDir(null)
  • GroupTrackStorage.tileRoot() → the same

⛔ BOTH NEED A CONTEXT. The accessor currently takes an optional one and falls back to the public path — which was correct while nothing had moved. ⚠ It now MUST have one, and these are object singletons: MapStateStore has no Context anywhere. This is the real design problem of tomorrow, not the redirects.

⭐ The manifest declares MeshUtilApplication. Its onCreate is the natural place to hand the accessor a context, and it runs before everything. ⚠ It is an UPSTREAM Meshtastic file, so that is a change outside the convoy tree — small, but worth knowing.

And the gate

Fred, 09-11: the authority gate can go. With no permission to check there is nothing to gate.

✅ SAFE TO REMOVE — background location is requested elsewhere. ConvoyScreen:1315 has its own bgLocationLauncher and :1396 checks the permission. So removing the gate does not lose location. ⚠ Verified 09-11, and it was the one thing that could have broken tracking silently.

⛔ BUT THE GATE IS THE NAV START DESTINATION. ConvoyNavigation.kt:61, and ConvoyRoutes lives in org.meshtastic.core.navigation — a DIFFERENT MODULE, outside the convoy tree. Removing the gate means changing where the app boots. ⚠ That is the edit most likely to break startup, and it wants doing on its own, first thing, not at the end of a long session.

⚠ And StartupHousekeeping.run() is called FROM the gate. Kill the gate and housekeeping needs a new home — it clears trails, runs the ALTERs, and installs the palette. ⛔ It cannot simply be dropped.
