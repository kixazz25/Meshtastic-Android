GroupTrack V2.5 — Release Notes
Updated: May 26, 2026
==========================================

NEW IN V2.5 — INDEPENDENT PLANNING MAP
==========================================

GroupTrack V2.5 introduces the Independent Planning Map,
a standalone map view for route planning, trail discovery,
and ride preparation — no radio connection required.

WHAT'S NEW
==========================================

PLANNING MAP
  Open the Planning Map from the main menu to explore
  trails, tracks, waypoints, and routes on satellite or
  topographic maps. Plan your ride before you leave home.

ARTIFACT DISPLAY (Trails, Tracks, Waypoints, Routes)
  Each artifact type has three display modes:

  OFF (gray)     — hidden from map
  ALL (blue)     — show everything in the current view
  SEL/EDIT (green) — open a list to select specific items

  Tap the green SEL/EDIT button to see all items in the
  current map area. Check the ones you want to see.
  Uncheck the rest. Close the panel and only your
  selected items display on the map. Zoom and pan freely —
  your selection stays locked.

  Tap ALL (blue) to go back to showing everything.
  Tap OFF (gray) to hide that type entirely.

COMBINED HEADER BAR
  One thin bar at the top of the Planning Map with:
  - BACK — return to main menu
  - SAT / TOPO / TOPO+ — switch map tiles instantly
  - QUEUES — view download progress

  Tap SAT for satellite imagery, TOPO for topographic,
  or TOPO+ for topographic with labels.

SEARCH
  Tap the small triangle button (▼) below the header to
  open the search bar. Type a city name, park, or trail
  area and press Enter on your keyboard. The map flies
  to that location. The search bar closes automatically
  and the keyboard dismisses.

  Examples: "Zion UT", "Moab", "Hurricane Cliffs"

TRAIL DISCOVERY
  The Planning Map shows all UGRC-mapped trails in the
  St. George / southern Utah region (5,732 trails).
  Trails appear as colored lines on the map. Tap any
  trail to see its name.

  When multiple trails overlap, tap the area and ALL
  nearby trail names appear in a popup for 4 seconds.
  No need to precisely tap a single thin line.

TRACK MANAGEMENT
  Import your recorded GPX tracks from the Downloads
  folder. View them on the map alongside trails.
  Use SEL/EDIT to pick specific tracks for display.

  Tracks from your rides appear as blue dashed lines.
  Tap any track to see its name.

WAYPOINT & ROUTE DISPLAY
  Import waypoints and routes from GPX files.
  Each type has its own display toggle and color.
  Waypoints appear as colored pins on the map.
  Routes appear as purple lines.

SELECT/EDIT PANEL
  When you tap SEL/EDIT, a list panel opens showing
  all items of that type in the current map area.

  You can:
  - Check/uncheck items to control what displays
  - Select All or Deselect All
  - Tap any item to open its detail panel
  - RENAME — change the display name
  - DELETE — remove from the database
  - CHANGE TYPE — (waypoints only) change the type
  - SHARE — email the item as a GPX attachment
  - EXPORT — save a GPX copy to Downloads

DOWNLOAD PANEL
  The download panel has three collapsible sections.
  Tap the section header to expand or collapse.

  MAP CONTROLS (collapsed by default)
    Flyover animation controls and download zoom level.

  DOWNLOAD AREA (expanded by default)
    Select what to download: tiles, trails, waypoints.
    Draw an area on the map, then tap START DOWNLOAD.
    Progress appears in the QUEUES panel.

  UPLOADS (collapsed by default)
    Upload tracks, routes, and waypoints to the cloud.
    (Coming in V2.6)

BACK BUTTON PROTECTION
  If you accidentally press Back, a confirmation dialog
  appears: "Leave Planning Map?" Your display settings
  (which types are on/off, which items are selected)
  are preserved when you return.

DRAGGABLE PANELS
  Several panels can be dragged to different positions
  on the screen. Touch and hold the panel header, then
  drag it to a new location. This lets you move panels
  out of the way for a better view of the map.

  Draggable panels:
  - Work With Artifacts panel
  - Download Queue panel
  - Select/Edit list panel

  Fixed elements:
  - Header bar (top)
  - Search bar (below header)

MAP TILES
  Three tile sources available:
  - SAT — Esri World Imagery (satellite photos)
  - TOPO — topographic contour lines
  - TOPO+ — topographic with transportation labels

  Downloaded tiles are available offline. The app
  automatically uses cached tiles when no internet
  connection is available.
