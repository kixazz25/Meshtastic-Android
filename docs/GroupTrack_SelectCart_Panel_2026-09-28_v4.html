Rules shown by this design

  • The ride's name and date head every panel.
  • The list is the radios heard on the ride's network — that is all a tablet knows. Everything shown about each comes from its own reports: callsign, last heard, distance, speed, battery — and, with the roles piece, its role and its check-in (green = its reports carry a check-in role; red = the default, counted as Rider).
  • Green = checked in; red = not checked in — no check-in heard from that radio, counted as Rider. A rider who has not checked in yet and a bare radio look the same from another tablet (the same reports), so there is one red state.
  • Every cart shows its current role.
  • Only Leader, Middle or Tail gunner see CHANGE ROLE and REMOVE FROM RIDE; any of them can change any role.
  • A special role moves: one holder each, always.
  • Both actions need CONFIRM, and go to every tablet in one roles-message format mechanics: roles piece.
