What changed

  • No checklist. One instruction at a time, over the map instead of covering it. Finished steps disappear rather than stacking up.
  • The state line is what the checklist was really for — a rider who panned away still sees what they have built.
  • Green means ready. Grey says the button exists but the ride is not viable yet. Absent said nothing at all.
  • The loop question answers itself. No finish point placed means a loop. Nobody promises a shape before seeing the ground.
  • Three marker kinds, told apart at a glance: blue square trailhead, red numbered pins, amber square finish.
