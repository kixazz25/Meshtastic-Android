=== RELEASE NOTES (internal, tester-facing draft) — Route Builder Stage 1 ===

NEW: Route planning (early access)
- Tap "+ ROUTE" on Work with Artifacts to open the Route+ toolbar.
- Tap "New Route", then "Add", then tap the map to drop points. A magenta
  line connects them as you go.
- "Undo" removes the last point. "EXIT" closes route mode.
- Pan and zoom work normally while placing points.
- Available on both the convoy map and the planning map.

KNOWN LIMITS (this build):
- Points land exactly where you tap (no trail-snapping yet — coming next).
- "Save completed" and "In-Progress" are visible but not active yet.
- "Draw" and "Suggest" methods are placeholders ("soon").

CHANGED:
- Route color is now magenta (was gold) so routes stand out against desert
  terrain in sunlight.
- QUEUES button repositioned to align with the N/Z18 indicator (no longer
  overlaps the map bar).

=== USER MANUAL ENTRY (draft) — Creating a Route ===

Creating a Route (early access)

1. Open the map (convoy or planning).
2. Open "Work with Artifacts" and tap "+ ROUTE". The Route+ toolbar appears.
3. Tap "New Route" to begin. The build controls become active.
4. Tap "Add", then tap the map where you want each point. A magenta line
   connects your points in order.
5. Use "Undo" to remove the most recent point.
6. Pan and zoom freely between taps to position the map.
7. Tap "EXIT" to leave route planning.

Notes:
- Routes display in magenta on the map.
- Trail-snapping (points automatically following nearby trails) and saving a
  finished route are coming in upcoming updates.
