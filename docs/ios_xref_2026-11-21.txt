HOW TO READ THE VERDICTS, and the warning that matters most:
  WORK        the body calls a named function
  REPORT      the body only messages or logs -- it ANSWERS and does not ACT
  OPENS-PANEL the body sets a flag a panel is bound to -- that IS the work (the panel is named)
  STATE-ONLY  the body assigns state that NO panel reads -- suspicious
  EMPTY       no body found
  *** A verdict covers ONE HOP. WORK says nothing about what the called function does. The row
