ADDENDUM 2026-06-03 (refinement to K correction) — drafts list is lightweight

Continue In-Progress (Work with Drafts) is a QUICK LIST -- no search/find needed.
Drafts are a small set (you finish or discard them; you don't accumulate many
incomplete routes). Just list name + last-modified, tap to resume.

Contrast, by storage reality:
  - CONTINUE IN-PROGRESS (Work with Drafts, private JSON) -> quick short list, NO
    search. A handful of draft files. Simple list composable.
  - COPY & MODIFY (artifact route list, routes table) -> NEEDS search/find. Completed
    routes accumulate (potentially many rows over time). List + search.

So search applies only to the completed-routes list, not the drafts list.
