PHASE 2 — the migration panel

Source Map Migration - SAT

Google Hybrid carries roads and place names inside the satellite imagery, so it needs one map store instead of three and shows street names even with no signal.

Your stored Esri tiles cannot be converted as they are. Road labels, place names and feature names will no longer show on them.

The separate name files those come from are removed either way, whichever option you choose.

DELETE AND START OVER - smallest and fastest

Clear these maps and rebuild around your tracks. Downloading along your tracks uses about two thirds less storage than boxed areas - you get the ground you actually ride and little else, and every future track brings its own maps. Frees 3 store(s), about 5507 MB.

This cannot be undone. There is no backup. Your download history for this slot is cleared too, so note any areas you want to redraw.

MOVE EVERYTHING TO THE NEW SOURCE - automatic

We convert your existing maps to Google Hybrid in the background, so road and place names come back across all of your coverage as quickly as possible - track maps first, then your areas underneath. Your maps stay usable the whole time.

All of the downloads are submitted at once and run as background tasks. Every image is slowly replaced from the new source until your whole map has moved over.

I'LL MOVE TRACKS AND AREAS ON MY SCHEDULE

Nothing is downloaded or removed. Your existing maps stay exactly as they are, so you always have something to fall back on. Refresh ground as you need it from the downloads panel - by track, or by drawing an area, with Replace tiles ticked. Downloading by track is the lighter option.

Not the best choice if you depend on road and place names - ground you have not refreshed stays without them.

⚠ What the render exposes

⛔ 1 — Phase 2 is far too long for an AlertDialog

Roughly 320 words in a Material3 dialog body. It will render as a tall scrolling block on a phone, with the three buttons below the fold on first view — so a user sees a wall of text and no visible actions.

⭐ This is the strongest argument yet for making it a full screen rather than a dialog. The content is a decision page, not a confirmation.

⚠ Short of that: cut each option to one sentence and move the detail behind the button, or drop the two "not the best choice / removed either way" lines, which are qualifiers rather than information.

⛔ 2 — Three stacked buttons in confirmButton will look wrong

Material3 expects one or two actions laid out horizontally. Three TextButtons in a Column render right-aligned and stacked, which reads as an accidental layout rather than a choice list.

⭐ The options are already described in the body, so the buttons are repeating text the user just read. Making them selectable rows in the body with a single CONTINUE action would fix both this and the length problem.

⚠ 3 — The greyed middle option is the informative one

You said nobody should make a delete decision without knowing the other options. As rendered, the user can only pick delete everything or do nothing — the option that makes the delete decision informed is the one they cannot choose.

⭐ It reads better once delete-by-track exists and all three are live. That is one function, copied from enqueueDelete.

⚠ 4 — Smaller things

  • "142880" has no thousands separator — it is interpolated raw.
  • "3 store(s)" — the (s) construction appears twice.
  • "about 5507 MB" — 5.5 GB would read better at this size.
  • The option headings are plain body text, same weight as everything else, so they do not stand out as headings on device.

⭐ What is right

  • Title is now honest — Source Map Migration, matching ConvoySourceMigration and maps/source_changes/.
  • The advisory leads — label loss is stated before any option is presented.
  • "Removed either way" heads off the obvious follow-up.
  • Each option names its action, and each has a distinct reason to be chosen.
  • Phase 1 is non-dismissible, phase 3 is — the decision cannot be escaped, the receipt can.
