The gap polyline (POLYLINE-2026-10-20) -- the design as built

  • Lead side (GroupTrack): dead air = the time the LEAD hears NO other cart (one cart heard = the hops relay to the rest). After ≥ 60 s of it (Fred 14:45: "a good even number" = six broadcasts), the first report heard from another cart sends ONE polyline: 20 points at equal time steps across [last heard .. heard again] from the lead's own radio fixes (option 1), plus one repeat at the next report heard ≥ 10 s later. Fewer than 2 own fixes in the window = nothing sent (logged). The lead = checked in as Leader, or the cart locked as lead at REC. Pop-up "Gap polyline sent: m:ss of dead air".
  • Receive, three layers (Fred 14:27 -- keep GroupTrack's message out of the mesh layer): core/data TakShapeInbox (NEW, generic: ANY TAK drawn shape, no GroupTrack knowledge; POLYTEST's GtPolylineInbox retired -- renamed *.removed_POLYLINE-2026-10-20, uncommitted, delete at clean-up) → comm layer MeshCommMessaging recognises u-d-f + uid GT-gap- → CommEvent.PolylineReceived (NEW, additive) → GroupTrack onGapPolylineReceived: pop-up; the LEAD's polyline with REC on → TrackEntry.Poly → LeadCartProcess.onGapPolyline → TrackWriter.pin. The repeat is recognised (sender + start) and not applied twice.
  • Pin (Fred 14:02-14:04, 14:47): the polyline REPLACES -- never a second line. Kept: the lead track up to the LAST REAL point heard before the window; removed: everything inferred after it; the 20 points joined in order by the fill rules (displayed route → known tracks → saved routes → trails → straight); the reconnect point joined to the first real point after the window; everything after stays. A point more than 50 m off the displayed route (or off every known line near it) is a DOT, never joined ("one point off turns the path into angles").
  • Radio setup: rebroadcast mode = ALL is now managed at check-in (written + verified: 25 checks); NONE / CORE_PORTNUMS_ONLY would stop relaying or drop TAK.
  • Desk test: LF loop 10,845 m pinned vs 10,768 m real (4 dots); afternoon 10,954 vs 11,115 (2 dots).
  • Road test 15:30-15:47 (Droid 1 leading the loop, Droid 2 parked): Droid 2 last heard the lead 15:29:01; the lead's own dead air 15:30:19 → 15:36:18 = 359 s; sent 15:36:18 (20 points, 202 bytes), received 2.1 s later (the clock difference within that), pinned with 10 of 20 as dots; repeat 15:36:38 recognised. All 20 points lie on the road Droid 1 drove (19 within 0-2 m, 1 at 22 m between fixes). The 10 dots = where this afternoon's loop left this morning's saved tracks (7 points 66-168 m from them; no route displayed) -- the rule worked as written; proposal (Fred to decide): with NO route displayed, a polyline point joins straight (it is the lead's real position).
