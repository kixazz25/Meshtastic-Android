GroupTrack — Quick Start

Rel 2.7c · the common jobs, step by step. Tap a task to open it; each step links to the User Manual section that explains that screen, and every screen's HELP opens that same section.

GroupTrack 2.7c has one map. Everything is reached from the buttons around its edge: MENU top left, the ride button top centre, a column down the right side, and the bottom bar. Two documents come with it: this Quick Start — how to get a job done — and the User Manual — what every screen does and how to use it. What is new is in the release notes.

The words we use. You go on a ride, you follow a route, and you record your track. Trails are on the ground, routes are planned, tracks are recorded — and a ride brings them together. All of them, and waypoints and trailheads: The words GroupTrack uses.
1 · First start

Install GroupTrack and set it up once.

  1. Install GroupTrack from Google Play and open it. (Before the map: first start)
  2. Fill in your rider profile: your callsign, email, vehicle and colour. MENU › Account.
  3. Load the trails for your state: Map › Import trails by state. Riding in Utah? Then Map › Assign Utah private property.
  4. Riding with a mesh radio? MENU › Settings › turn Group awareness on. Riding alone with the tablet, leave it off.
Imported tracks before 5 October 2026? Run Map › Create trails from tracks once. Running it again does no harm.
2 · Set up the map

Make the map show what matters for your ride.

  1. Choose the picture: Map: SAT, TOPO or TOPO+ on the right side.
  2. Choose what is drawn — trails, tracks, routes, waypoints, public or private land: FILTER.
  3. Find out what a line means, or change its colour: MAP KEY, then STYLE › on its row.
  4. Learn to move around the map: Map gestures and Touching the map.
3 · Plan a route

Lay out where the group will ride.

  1. Tap Add Route on the bottom bar.
  2. Let GroupTrack design it: AI route design — there must be a trail within ½ mile (2,640 ft) of where you start. Or draw it yourself: Build it myself.
  3. Save it. A trailhead within 0.1 mile (528 ft) of the first point is reused; otherwise a new one is made.
  4. Old unfinished routes in the way? Clear leftover routes.
4 · Get the maps before you lose signal

Download the maps you will need where there is no cell service.

  1. Along a route or track: Map › Download maps along tracks. For a whole area: Map › Download maps by area.
  2. Watch it finish: the first line of the Map menu, or DETAILS › the queue.
  3. Check where your maps reach: FILTER › OFF-GRID MAPS › tick Available.
  4. Leave Download settings › Replace existing tiles off, unless you mean to change the imagery.
5 · Create a ride and send it

One ride holds the route, trailhead, date and time, the maps and the radio setup.

  1. Rides › Create a ride. Start from a route or a track; GroupTrack looks for trailheads within ½ mile (2,640 ft).
  2. Complete every item — a ride cannot be sent until it is complete.
  3. Rides › My rides › Send. An email opens with the ride attached: add the riders' addresses and send.
6 · Get a ride someone sent you

Open the ride and GroupTrack sets everything up.

  1. Tap the ride attachment in the email and choose Open with GroupTrack. Watch the route, the trailhead, the offline maps and the mesh radio setup for the ride being created.
  2. Saved the file instead? Rides › Import a ride — the section shows how to find it in a busy Downloads folder.
  3. Riding with a radio: put the ride's settings on it — RADIO SETUP › Configure radio for rides.
7 · Ride on your own

One rider, no radio: the tablet's GPS places your cart.

  1. Set Mode to Android only.
  2. Tap REC to record your track.
  3. Keep your cart in view: CENTRE ON ME. Read your speed, heading and battery on the readout.
  4. Finish: Ending the ride.
8 · Ride as a group

Several riders, each with a mesh radio: every cart on the map, no cell service needed.

  1. Set Mode to Group, and connect your radio: GRP Awareness.
  2. Tap CHK IN and choose your ride (or No scheduled ride and name your track) and your role. Checking in does not start recording — the button then reads REC: tap it to record.
  3. See every cart, and change roles: Rides › View checked-in carts.
  4. Switch the readout between your cart and the group: View: My Cart / Group (the readout).
9 · After the ride

Finish cleanly and help us improve GroupTrack.

  1. End the ride. Your track is saved, and ground you rode where no trail is published becomes a rider trail by itself.
  2. Send the ride's statistics to GroupTrack: Rides › Email last ride statistics (Rides).
  3. See how well the radios heard each other: MENU › Settings › Ride statistics.
10 · Something wrong?

Help is on every screen.

  1. HELP, top right of every panel, opens the manual at the job you are in.
  2. ISSUE, beside it, captures the screen and emails it to us — say in the email what went wrong.
  3. A trail missing from the map: MENU › Settings › Check a trail.
  4. What changed in this version: the release notes.
Map › Resync tracks is a repair tool — use it only when GroupTrack support asks.
