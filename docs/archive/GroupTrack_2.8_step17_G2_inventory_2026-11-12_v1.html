1. What G2 is

The things the app opens with: where its files live, its settings, the rider's profile and networks, the session, the state/agency data list, and the start-up jobs. 17 files, 2,987 lines. Every one of them reaches Android directly (Context, SharedPreferences, File, android.database, Log), which is why G2 is first: the later groups call into it.

3. The finding that shapes G2 — paths are already socketed THREE times

Steps 15–16 each built their own "where are my files" interface: GtImportPaths (imports: groupTrackRoot, tracksDir, dir(name), cacheDir, filesDir), RideFiles (rides: ridesDir, stagingDir, child, sibling, name) and GtQueuePlatform.recipesDir() (16b). Each Android version wraps GroupTrackStorage. G2a makes ONE storage socket in grouptrack-shared — GtStorage: root, dir(name), tracksDir, tileRoot, cacheDir, filesDir, isInternal — with one Android implementation over GroupTrackStorage (unchanged) and, at step 18, one iPad implementation (the app's Documents/GroupTrack). The three existing interfaces then take their answers from it, so a path can only be worked out one way on each platform (the V3 rule: produced identically).
