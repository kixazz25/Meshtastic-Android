4. Risks

  • The tick is the heart of group riding — any difference shows as a cart that jumps, vanishes or is renamed. Mitigation: shadow (D2), one step per commit, both droids.
  • Field distance: Fred is in NH without carts to space apart — moving-position checks are limited until January (Utah). Stationary carts, roles, names, link loss and Android-only mode can all be checked on the bench.
  • Time units: Meshtastic's lastHeard and position.time are seconds; Member carries milliseconds — the conversion is the comm side's (one place).
