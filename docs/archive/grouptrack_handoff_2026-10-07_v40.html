9. Lessons (for the Living Master)

  • Ask what the rider actually did before building fixes. Ten rounds of compare/batch/leftover fixes were built before the cause (map key off + trailhead on a track) came from looking at his map. The first question for a single-device failure: version installed, and a screenshot of the screen.
  • Load and test locally before Play (Fred: "this is what happens when we do not load and test locally — a mess"). Now a gate in the procedure.
  • Fixes go where the rider is: a tool that needs leaving the screen destroys the state it should capture; a button in a dialog the flow never reaches does not exist.
  • Silent exits are bugs: two of the AI search's five zero-route exits only logged; and 0 routes reopens the AI panel at its first page. TRAILHEADNEAR stops the main cause up front; the silent restart itself remains (patch_aiempty was dropped by choice).
  • Tracks become trails where no trail exists — so a trailhead "on a track" is on a trail, and the map key can hide it.
