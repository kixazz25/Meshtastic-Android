GroupTrack V2.5 — Release Notes
What's new · Field Test Release · Updated 2026-06-21 · grouptrack.org
⚠ Updating from an earlier version? Read this first.

Install as an UPDATE — do NOT uninstall the old version and install this as a new install. Let the app update in place through the Play Store. Uninstalling and then clean-installing will break the installation, and you will have to reach out for help to recover it.

After the update, on first launch the trail/track database is rebuilt. So:

  • Re-import your trails — open Work with Artifacts → Import Trails and import UGRC Utah Trails again (brings back trails + trailhead waypoints).
  • Your recorded tracks come back automatically from your saved GPX files on launch. If any track is missing, open the track import screen and tap RESYNC TRACKS.
  • Your GPX track files and downloaded map tiles are not affected by the update.

If you already did a clean install and it’s broken: the fix is to remove the two GroupTrack database files from /sdcard/Documents/GroupTrack/ before relaunching — contact the organizer for help, as this is a manual step.

What's new in this build

Find anything, then fit it to your screen
One search on either map covers Areas, Tracks, Routes, Trails, and Waypoints. Tap a result to open its detail, then tap FIT to fly the map to it and frame it on its own.
Benefit: jump straight to the trail or track you mean, instead of hunting the map.
Manual: Universal search · the detail panel · FIT
One detail panel for every artifact
Trails, tracks, routes, and waypoints all open the same detail panel — from a search result, a select/edit row, or by tapping the artifact on the map. Rename, change type, share, or delete from one place.
Benefit: the same simple panel everywhere, so you always know where the controls are.
Manual: The artifact detail panel
Trail type and color, on the trail itself
Each trail’s classification — OHV / Road-Concurrent, Hiking & Biking, Hiking-Only, Paved Shared Use, and so on — shows as a colored Carto Type band in its detail panel, and now also in the popup when you tap a trail on the map.
Benefit: tell at a glance which trails are open to your vehicle.
Manual: Carto Type · the CartoCode color legend
Refresh trail data without starting over
In Trail Sources, an already-imported source now stays selectable. Pick it again and run it to refresh trail classification and details on the trails you already have — your geometry and recorded tracks are untouched. A live recap counts records as they process.
Benefit: pull updated trail info anytime, without re-downloading from scratch.
Manual: Trail Sources
Help on both maps
The ? button that opens the manual and release notes is on the Convoy Map as well as the Planning Map.
Benefit: help is one tap away wherever you are.
Manual: Convoy & Planning maps
Clearer direction arrows on tracks
Direction-of-travel arrows on tracks are evenly spaced on screen and stay readable as you zoom, drawn in the same neon green as the track.
Benefit: read which way a track runs at a glance, even on long tracks.
Manual: Tracks
Each map remembers where you left it
The Convoy and Planning maps each restore their own last view and what you had selected, independently of each other.
Benefit: pick up exactly where you were, on the right map.
Manual: Maps
Build routes that follow real trails
Tap to drop points and each one snaps to the nearest trail or track; tap away from a trail to place a free point. Name it and save (early access).
Benefit: lay out a route along established paths in seconds.
Manual: Creating a Route

Also in V2.5

Bring your own trail data
Trail data is no longer bundled with the app. You import the regions where you ride — by Source (pick a land agency) or by Area (draw a box). One-time, a few minutes on WiFi; it’s permanent on your device after that.
Benefit: trails for the places you actually ride, across many states and agencies.
Manual: Trail Sources
Faster maps with the spatial database
Trails and tracks load only for the part of the map you’re looking at, from an on-device spatial database. Tracks sync in automatically from your GPX files on launch.
Benefit: instant pan and zoom even with tens of thousands of trails.
Manual: Trail & track display
Work with Artifacts, now icon-driven
A right-edge icon column on both maps gives you search, Work with Artifacts, and help. The panel opens expanded and collapses closed — the old always-on bar is gone.
Benefit: more map, controls one tap away when you want them.
Manual: The right-edge icon column
More map sources, and download controls
16 tile sources (Google, Esri, USGS, OpenTopo, OSM, Thunderforest, CartoDB) assignable to your SAT / TOPO / TOPO+ buttons, with API-key management. The download queue supports hold, resume, cancel, and a settable number of simultaneous downloads.
Benefit: the basemaps you prefer, and control over big offline downloads.
Manual: Map Sources · Offline Maps & Downloads
