AI Design
Rides are worked out from the trail data for this area, the natural features on it — springs, summits, cliffs, cones — and the points of interest. Two ways to ask.
Explore the area
You give a starting point and how long you want to be out. GroupTrack picks everything else — it looks for the rides that take in the most features for the distance you have.
Use this when you do not know the area, or want to be shown something you would not have found.
Include places I choose
The same, but you drop the places that have to be in the ride. GroupTrack fits in as many as the distance allows and tells you which it could not.
Expect fewer options back — every place you add is one more thing the ride has to satisfy.

⚠ It has not seen the ground

GroupTrack knows a trail is there. It does not know how wide it is, what the surface is like, or what is on it — that is not in the trail data. Zoom in on the satellite view before you commit, and use the width tool to check your machine will fit.
