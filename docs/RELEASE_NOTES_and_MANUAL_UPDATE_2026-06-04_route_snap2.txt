NEW: Route planning — build and save a route (early access)
- Open "Work with Artifacts" and tap "+ ROUTE" to open the Route+ toolbar.
- Tap "New". You're asked to NAME the route (a name is filled in for you —
  edit it or keep it), then tap "Start".

  trail or track within range, so your route follows real trails instead of
  straight lines between taps. Tap away from any trail and the point stays

USER MANUAL ENTRY — Creating a Route (replaces the Stage-1 provisional entry)
================================================================

Creating a Route (early access)

A route is a planned path you build by tapping the map. Points snap to nearby
trails and tracks so the route follows real ground, and you save it with a name
for later use.

1. Open the map (convoy or planning).
2. Open "Work with Artifacts" and tap "+ ROUTE". The Route+ toolbar appears.
3. Tap "New". A "Name this route" box appears with a suggested name — accept it
   or type your own — then tap "Start".
4. Tap "Add" (it turns green), then tap the map where you want each point.
   - Each point snaps to the nearest trail or track if one is close by, so your
     route follows established paths.
   - Tap away from any trail to place a free point exactly where you tapped.
   - A magenta line connects your points in order.
5. Use "Undo" to remove the most recent point.
6. Pan and zoom freely between taps to position the map.
7. Tap "Save" to store the route. It is saved on your device, redraws on the map
   in magenta, and appears in Work with Artifacts → Routes under the name you
   gave it.
8. Tap "Discard"/"EXIT" to leave without saving.

Notes:
- Routes display in magenta. Saved routes load from your device's spatial
  database the same viewport-aware way trails and tracks do.
- Saving a half-finished route to resume later ("In Progress"), editing a saved
  route's geometry, and renaming from the Routes list are coming in upcoming
  updates.
- "Draw" and "Suggest" route methods are planned ("soon").
