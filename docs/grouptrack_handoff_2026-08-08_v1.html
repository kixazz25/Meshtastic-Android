2 · What happened yesterday, and why

  1. Option 2 ran with corridors selected. The delete never executed — optTracks was false at the handler though the box appeared ticked.
  2. Corridor tiles stayed on disk, so the area pass read them as area coverage and queued 172 jobs to re-download that ground as rectangles.
  3. The panel never dismissed: refreshEnqueued was set only at the end of the coroutine.
  4. Entries were cleared while WorkManager still held the jobs. Workers take everything from inputData and never consult the queue, so they ran 20 minutes at 0 tiles.
  5. The panel returns early when the queue is empty, so RELEASE and CANCEL were off screen. Force-stop rescheduled them.
  6. Clearing app data was the only exit. The spatial DB went with it.

⭐ THE MEASUREMENT THAT MATTERS: 30 tracks swept up as areas cost 2M+ tiles on one source; 89 tracks as corridors cost 1.5M across three. Corridors were designed for ~90% fewer tiles and that holds. The delete is not space reclamation — it is what stops corridor ground being re-covered as boxes. It is now mandatory whenever corridors are selected.

5 · The estimator was 8-10x long

The panel said Aug 12 5 AM (~86h) for 1.5M tiles. Real rate ~140,000/hour ≈ 10h.

Three compounding causes: the rate came from ONE entry (a per-slot rate while 3-4 slots ran); averaging two entries did not fix it because concurrent windows overlap; and createdAt is queue time, so a job that waited behind 87 others carried hours of waiting in its elapsed.

⭐ Replaced with your formulation: total tiles completed ÷ elapsed since the earliest job started. That IS the aggregate rate by construction — concurrency cannot fool it, and it absorbs stalls, holds and slot changes. Same arithmetic as the yardstick ledger, so the two finally agree.

7 · Method notes

  • ⛔⛔ A GUARD THAT SEARCHES FOR THE OLD CODE FINDS IT IN YOUR OWN COMMENT. Three times today. ⭐ Fixed structurally: every patch script now self-tests each guard string against every replacement block before shipping. Describe the old code, never quote it.
  • ⛔ MULTI-LINE ANCHORS FROM sed OUTPUT ARE UNRELIABLE — blank lines between statements are invisible there. Two anchors failed on it. Use cat -A, or anchor on one unique line.
  • ⚠ A FILE-WIDE GUARD FLAGS CORRECT CODE. Checking active.size had vanished flagged a legitimate call. Guard the expression, not the token.
  • ⚠ A WINDOW ANCHORED ON THE FIRST OCCURRENCE LANDS IN THE WRONG PLACE — the finally check searched from the option ROW, hundreds of lines above the branch.
  • ⚠ A PATCH INTRODUCING A COMPOSE SYMBOL MUST ADD ITS IMPORT IN THE SAME EDIT. Twice today a patch named the imports in its own output and shipped without them.
  • ⭐⭐ THE REASSEMBLY WAS THE HARD PART, NOT THE PIECES. Every component was already built and proven. Calling them in order from a different screen introduced two defects and cost a device rebuild.
