2. Principles (Fred, 10-18 to 10-21)

  • One map. The planning map is folded in; the Ride Planning / Back to Ride switches are retired.
  • A launch layer over the existing features. Each process gets ONE first screen that takes every value it needs (defaults from the ride and settings), then runs to the end with no further screen prompts. The only stop kept is the confirm before something that cannot be undone.
  • Two layouts. Group adds a lot of complexity and is used by some. Android only is the default; Group adds the Group button, CHECK IN, the GROUP readout, other carts and the lead track. One switch decides which.
  • Riders ride in landscape and pan in portrait. In landscape, panels open as a side panel so the map stays in view.
  • Every control shows an icon and its text on an opaque background, readable on any map. No hover, no long-press meanings.
  • North up only. Leaflet cannot rotate, and Hybrid labels are baked into the imagery (heading-up needs a rotating map engine: not 2.7c).
  • Show/hide is FILTER, how lines look is KEY: as onX and Gaia do, the filter is on every view.
