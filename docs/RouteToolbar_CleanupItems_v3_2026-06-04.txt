----------------------------------------------------------------
HANDLE BUG — DIAGNOSED (symptom: "handle opens the accordion")
----------------------------------------------------------------
Observed: tapping the ✥ grip handle toggles the accordion (collapse/expand)
instead of dragging the panel.

Root cause (two halves of one hit-testing problem):
  - The ✥ handle has ONLY pointerInput{ detectDragGestures }. A detectDrag
    only fires on DRAG motion, NOT on a tap. So a tap on the handle is not
    consumed by the handle.
  - The handle is the first child of the header Row; the title beside it has
    Modifier.weight(1f).clickable { minimized = !minimized }. The unconsumed
    tap falls through / bleeds to the weighted title's clickable -> accordion
    toggles.
  - The glyph is tiny, so the finger likely lands partly on the title hit
    area too.
Net: the drag never moves the bar (item 2) AND taps near the handle trigger
the accordion. Same class of tap-vs-gesture collision as the QUEUES button.

----------------------------------------------------------------
RECOMMENDED FIX (snap-2 patch) — DROP THE DRAG
----------------------------------------------------------------
Strongest option (Option B from v2, now reinforced by this bug):
  - Remove the detectDragGestures + offsetX/offsetY + .offset{} entirely.
  - Header becomes purely: title + chevron, both .clickable -> accordion.
  - Remove the ✥ handle, OR keep it as a non-interactive visual grip (no
    pointerInput at all) so it can't eat or bleed taps.
  - Result: no drag, no collision, no tap-bleed. Accordion is the only
    real-estate control — sufficient on a ~380px phone panel.

If drag is genuinely wanted later: it must be a real draggable with its own
consumed tap+drag handling (separate pointerInput that consumes taps), placed
so it doesn't overlap the title's clickable hit area. More work; not worth it
now per Fred ("not important").
