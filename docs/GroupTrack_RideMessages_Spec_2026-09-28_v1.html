Ride messages — process spec (stubs)

2026-09-28 v1 · Fred. Formats and behaviour only — the mechanics (encoding, transport, parsing, storage) are written with the roles piece. STUB marks each part not yet built.

The rules — for every ride message
  1. Sent only at check-in, or on a confirmed action. Nothing is sent while riding — no periodic packets.
  2. One format for all messages: a type, the ride's id, the sender, and the type's own fields.
  3. Every message carries the ride's id; a tablet ignores messages for a ride it is not checked in to.
  4. Travels on the ride's radio network, the same network the group rides on.
  5. Changes that affect others need CONFIRM before they are sent (role changes, removals).
  6. Only the Leader, Middle or Tail gunner may send ROLE_CHANGE and REMOVE_FROM_RIDE; only the Leader sends NOTES_UPDATE and NOTES_REPLY.
  7. The latest message wins: each tablet applies messages in the order they were sent (the sender's time), so a late, stale message never overrides a newer one.

1 · ROLE_CHANGE STUB

TriggerSELECT CART → a cart → CHANGE ROLE → CONFIRM
Sent byThe Leader, Middle or Tail gunner (any of them, for any of the three roles)
Fieldstarget (the cart receiving the role) · role (Leader / Middle / Tail gunner / Rider) · swapWith (the role's previous holder, who takes the target's old role)
ReceiversEvery tablet updates both carts' roles in its list and on its map. The target's tablet writes the new role to its radio; the previous holder's tablet writes its new role to its radio. The tick follows the latest change.
RulesA special role moves — always exactly one Leader, one Middle, one Tail gunner. Announced on every tablet ("lost again made Jim B the Leader").

2 · REMOVE_FROM_RIDE STUB

TriggerSELECT CART → a cart → REMOVE FROM RIDE → CONFIRM
Sent byThe Leader, Middle or Tail gunner
Fieldstarget (the cart removed)
ReceiversEvery tablet drops the cart from the ride's list. The target's radio is not changed.
RulesA removed cart holding a special role — to be decided with the roles piece (e.g. removal refused until its role is moved first).

3 · NOTES_UPDATE STUB

TriggerThe leader's check-in, when he changed the ride's Leader's Notes
Sent byThe Leader — once, and only if the notes changed
Fieldsnotes (the ride's Leader's Notes, in full)
ReceiversEvery tablet updates its copy of the ride's notes (the database) and shows them in MY CART / MY GROUP.
RulesThe notes are the ride's record; after sending, the leader's check-in is the only place they change. Typical use: the day's radio frequency, a changed meeting point.

4 · NOTES_REQUEST STUB

TriggerEvery rider's check-in
Sent byThe rider checking in
Fieldsnone beyond the common format
ReceiversThe leader's tablet answers with NOTES_REPLY. Other tablets ignore it.
RulesNo answer (leader not checked in, or out of range): the rider keeps the notes from the ride file, and gets the new ones from the leader's NOTES_UPDATE when he checks in.

5 · NOTES_REPLY STUB

TriggerA NOTES_REQUEST reaching the leader's tablet
Sent byThe Leader's tablet — automatically, once per request
Fieldsto (the rider who asked) · notes (in full)
ReceiversThe asking rider's tablet updates its copy of the ride's notes.

Together, every rider ends up with the latest notes
