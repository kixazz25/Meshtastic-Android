🔴 ISSUE 2 — the horizon. Not a bug; a design gap with a 4% fix.

At z16 the screen fills. At z15 and z14 the corners go white. ⭐ The corridor is a half-mile band and the view widens faster than the band does.

⚠ Each zoom is an INDEPENDENT tile set. Having z18 gives you nothing at z13 — they are separate downloads of the same ground at different resolutions. "Less detail" does not mean "less data": it means more ground per tile, so a wider view, so more of it outside your coverage.

⭐⭐ The fix Fred arrived at — a horizon measured in TILES, not miles

buffer(z) = H × tileWidth(z)
tiles(z)  = (trackLength / tileWidth(z) + 1) × (2H + 1)

H = 2  ->  always two tiles of map beyond the track, at every zoom

Cost: 4%. For the 42-mile Parowan track — 10,791 tiles today at half a mile everywhere — H=2 with z16-18 left unchanged comes to 11,214 tiles, and it fills the view all the way down to z9.

⚠ corridorTiles already loops per zoom with bufferDeg as a parameter — the buffer just becomes a function of z. ⚠ Existing stores keep their shape; only new downloads get the horizon.
