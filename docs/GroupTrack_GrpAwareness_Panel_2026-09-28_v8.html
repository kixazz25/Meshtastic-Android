The design

  • Top: the Connected to box — the radio's long name with DISCONNECT as its action; empty when nothing is connected.
  • Available radios — with no connection, "Select a radio from the list to connect" above the list.
  • Radios by their long name (the rider's name for the radio, e.g. "lost again — T1000-E"); the node id and the Bluetooth name small underneath. A radio never connected yet shows its Bluetooth name until its long name is known.
  • One radio at a time: while one is connected, CONNECT is greyed on every other — DISCONNECT first. The connected radio lives in the Connected to box; on DISCONNECT it moves into Available radios.
  • FORGET on each available radio (never the one in use), confirmed: removed from GroupTrack and from Android's Bluetooth pairings.
  • The title is red until a radio is connected, then green — like the label on the map.
  • New radios: while the panel is open it scans (the app's own scan); a radio this tablet has never paired through the app appears in Available radios marked NEW TO THIS ANDROID with PAIR — PAIR pairs it through the app (the radio's PIN), then it is an ordinary available radio. (Pairing in Android's settings alone does not make a radio appear in the app.)
  • Three actions side by side at the bottom: Select a ride and apply settings to the radio · Radio settings (⚠ experienced mesh operators only — the web client with internet, after disconnecting; the app's settings without) · Meshtastic side menu (temporary). ✕ at the top closes. (ADD A RADIO is gone — new radios appear by themselves.)
