# GroupTrack weekly posts — ten weeks

One panel a week, in order. Each panel says *n of 10*, and the orange trail along the bottom runs from one week into the next.
Every post ends with the same link:

**Get GroupTrack Off-Road Navigation on Google Play:**
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.grouptrack.android&hl=en_US

The words, every week: you go on a **ride**, follow its **route**, and record your **track**.

---

## Week 1 — Create a ride. (`GroupTrack_weekly_2026-10-02_01of10.png`)

GroupTrack 2.7 is on Google Play, and it's built around one idea: riding together starts with a ride.
Tap a route on the planning map, tap CREATE RIDE, set the date and time, and send it. One ride package holds the route, its trailhead, every map you'll need offline and the radio configuration.

## Week 2 — Open a ride in one tap. (`…_02of10.png`)

Your riding partners get the invite by email and tap it once. GroupTrack loads the route, the trailhead and the maps for the ride — so everyone arrives with the same prep, ready to ride off grid.

## Week 3 — Check in. Your radio is set. (`…_03of10.png`)

At the trailhead, everyone checks in to the ride. GroupTrack sets each rider's mesh radio up for the group — nobody configures anything by hand — and the whole group is on one map, even with no cell service.

## Week 4 — Riding together starts with a ride. (`…_04of10.png`)

No more night before the ride spent downloading an area and hoping the trail goes where you think it does. Build the ride once, send it, and everyone shows up with the same maps, the same route and the same meeting point.

## Week 5 — Turn a route into a ride. (`…_05of10.png`)

Any route you've built becomes a ride in a minute. Tap it on the planning map, tap CREATE RIDE, and the ride form opens with the route, its trailhead and its picture already in place.

## Week 6 — Turn a track into a ride. (`…_06of10.png`)

Rode something great last season? Tap the track you recorded and tap CREATE RIDE. Your track becomes the route — every turn kept — and you can invite the group to ride it with you.

## Week 7 — One ride package. (`…_07of10.png`)

A GroupTrack ride is a bundle: the route, its trailhead, the date and time, every map the ride needs, and the radio configuration. Sent in one click, opened in one tap.

## Week 8 — Tell it where to go. It builds the routes. (`…_08of10.png`)

Say where you're starting, how far you want to go and how fast you ride. GroupTrack searches your trail network and lays out several complete routes side by side — pick one, and turn it into a ride.

## Week 9 — Every map you need, along your route, offline. (`…_09of10.png`)

GroupTrack downloads the satellite and topo maps along your route — not a whole box of country you'll never ride. You'll see the trail at every zoom, with no signal, which is most of where we ride.

## Week 10 — Bring your riding partners with you. (`…_10of10.png`)

That's how the community grows: one ride, one invite at a time. Send your next ride to a friend who hasn't tried GroupTrack yet — the invite has the download link in it.
