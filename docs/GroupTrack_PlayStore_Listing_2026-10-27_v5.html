Full description — 3078 / 4,000

An off-road trail app for side-by-side, UTV, ATV, dirt bike and Jeep riders. It holds the trails and the maps on your device, so it keeps working where there is no signal — which is most of where you ride.

New in 2.7c: one map Planning and riding now happen on one map. Every job is one button from its edge — Map, Add Route, Rides, Search, Radio Setup — and every step of that job sits behind it. It is laid out the way trail riders already work, so it feels like an old friend. Every screen has HELP, which opens the manual at the job you are doing.

Tell it the ride you want. Get routes worth taking. Say where you are starting, how long you want to ride and where you want to go. GroupTrack searches your trail network and comes back with up to six complete routes, side by side, so you can compare them and keep the ones you like. Or draw your own: tap points on the map and the route snaps to the real trail between them.

Each route arrives described: distance, riding time, and every summit, spring and landmark you will pass, in the order you will reach them, with the named trails it follows.

Make it a ride, and send it A ride is everything your group needs in one package: the route, its trailhead, the date and rollout time, the maps to have offline, and the radio setup. Send it by email. Your riders open it with one tap, and GroupTrack puts the route on their map, downloads the maps along it and sets up their radio.

It identifies new trails from your ride tracks Every app lets you import your old tracks. GroupTrack does something else with them. Where a ride of yours crosses ground no published survey covers, it becomes a trail — part of your network, searchable, and available to the route planner. One rider's collection of 88 tracks produced over 1,800 new trails covering 2,400 miles that no public source had. Import everything you have — onX, Gaia, anywhere — and every new ride you record adds to it.

Your whole group on one map, with no cell service Pair a Meshtastic radio and check in at the trailhead: every rider appears on your map in real time. No towers, no signal, no network subscription. The lead knows the tail is still coming; the tail knows nobody was left at a junction. Group span, rider count and who leads and who sweeps, on screen, all day.

Everything works offline Load your state's trails and download satellite or topo maps before you leave — by area, or along your routes. Plan, follow, record and track your group with the tablet in airplane mode.

Learn it in an afternoon A Quick Start walks through the ten jobs you will do most, each step a tap away from the manual section that explains it.

Honest about what it knows GroupTrack knows a trail is there. It does not know how wide it is, what the surface is like, or what is on it. Check the satellite view before you commit, and check access and ownership the way you would anywhere else.

First run Loading your home state downloads its full trail data from several sources — plan on half an hour. Do it on wifi, before the trip, not in the trailhead parking lot.

Changes from v4: the 2.7c One Map section; Create a Ride; "draw your own" (Build it myself snaps to the trail); the Quick Start is ten jobs, not 32 exercises; first run is "half an hour" for the home state.

What's new — 401 / 500

2.7c — One map. Planning and riding are now one map, with every job one button from its edge. HELP on every screen opens the new manual at the job you are doing, and a new Quick Start covers the ten common jobs. Radios now report every 30 m (98 ft): re-apply your radio setup. Ride statistics can be emailed to us after a ride. GRP Awareness asks to turn Bluetooth on, and offers a way to turn it off.
