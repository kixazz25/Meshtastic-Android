HANDLE BUG — CONFIRMED BEHAVIOR (the clincher)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Observed, state-dependent:
  - Accordion OPEN  -> dragging the ✥ handle MOVES the bar (works).
  - Accordion COLLAPSED -> tapping the handle OPENS the accordion
    (drag doesn't engage; tap bleeds to the title's clickable).

This confirms the hit-test collision: drag and accordion-tap fight over
the same pixels in the cramped 248dp header, and which one wins depends
on the layout state (open header has room for the drag to register;
collapsed header tightens so the tap lands on the title's
.clickable{ minimized = !minimized }).

Same root cause as the QUEUES button tap-vs-drag collision, new location.
You cannot reliably have BOTH "tap toggles accordion" and "drag moves bar"
sharing the header pixels — they steal each other's gestures.
