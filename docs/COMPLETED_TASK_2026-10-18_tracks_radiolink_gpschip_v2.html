4. Findings

What the logs proved today (evidence, not theory)

  1. No GPS fix was treated as a dead radio. LINKHB (10-05) made "talking" = my radio's own TAK report; a T1000-E sends that only with a fix. The REAL healthStep code run in a simulation (scratchpad hs18): a 5-min fix gap = 4 forced reconnects (before), 2 (HEALTHTRAFFIC, alone on the road), 0 (GPSNOTFATAL). Each forced reconnect is a Bluetooth teardown — the wedge of 10-16 / 10-18 morning.
  2. The phantom link is a Meshtastic fault. 14:04:32 Android reported the radio ending the connection (BluetoothGatt status=19); the app kept link=CONNECTED until our silence rule forced a reconnect. SILENCE30S now catches it: 30 s message, 45 s reconnect.
  3. Meshtastic's Bluetooth keep-alive is empty. AndroidRadioInterfaceService.keepAlive() sends a heartbeat every 30 s only over USB; NordicBleInterface.keepAlive() only logs. Nothing checks a Bluetooth radio. BLEHEARTBEAT patch written (not applied).
  4. Reconnect attempts stack up. When Billy beans came back (14:20:15) there were 8 configureMTU calls at once — one per earlier try. No harm proven; no fix yet.
  5. Billy beans sent no position 14:02–14:26 — neither tablet heard it; it was relaying fred&June to Droid 2 the whole time. Its first broadcast came 2 s after Fred's second manual reconnect (14:26:06 → 14:26:08): cause or coincidence UNKNOWN. A first manual reconnect at 14:24 was NOT followed by broadcasts.
  6. COTMSG is rate-limited by design (one log line per radio per minute, MeshCommMessaging.kt line 128); every received report becomes a CoT message on the board.
  7. The check-in lead works: Droid 2 received fred&June's TeamLead (15:32:05, 16:38:26) → LEADBOARD lead = !23dec46a.
  8. The lead track trim fails on a loop. LeadTrack.shownSegments cuts at the LAST lead point within 50 m of my cart; when the lead comes back to where I am, his newest point is beside me and the whole loop is hidden (Droid 2: 1→7 segments, then 0 at 15:35:14 / 16:41:05; Fred saw ~100 ft). OPEN.
