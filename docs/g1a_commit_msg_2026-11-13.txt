TWO DELIBERATE DEPARTURES FROM G2f
  Order is COMPARED, not sorted away. 2.7e builds `tiles` with mutableListOf
  and nested for(z)for(x)for(y), so order is fixed by the source, not by a hash
  or the filesystem; it is part of the behaviour. G2f sorted because ITS
  collections came from set iteration and listFiles(). Nothing here does.

  Mercator edges are in the sweep on purpose. At 89.999 degrees tan(rad) runs
  away and 2.7e clamps nothing, so both sides must run away identically.
  Faithfulness, not correctness -- a defect both sides reproduce is a PASS.
  It came back clean on Apple silicon, as did the negative-x+y modulo.
