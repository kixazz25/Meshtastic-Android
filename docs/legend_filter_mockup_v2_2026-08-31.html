Blue is rideable and the shade carries confidence — OHV is stated in the data, shape-only means nothing was said and it is in on shape alone. Thin lines are not for riding.

Private is a filter, not a colour: a private forestry road keeps its blue and simply disappears when Land is set to Public.

Categories with nothing in them drop out of the legend entirely — a state with no OHV-designated trails shows no OHV row.

Category toggles work inside the slice. Set Land to Public and hiking & biking falls from 24,414 to 5,618 whatever its own toggle says; the count shows visible over total so it is obvious why.
