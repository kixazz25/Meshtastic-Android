⭐ THE DROID-vs-iPAD DIVERGENCE — QUESTION CLOSED, CAUSE NAMED

Fred, 17:05: “routes are more disparate on iPad… more features and point of interest on iPad search?” MEASURED: no. The two devices produce identical networks — 24 features, 2,891 trails, 4,218 junctions on both. Claude’s first answer (“the iPad is better at finding features”) was wrong and is withdrawn.

THE ONLY DELTA: the Droid’s filter is land_status IN ('PUBLIC','PRIVATE'); the iPad’s is IN ('PUBLIC','PRIVATE','RESTRICTED'). That decides whether one restricted trail’s open pieces enter the network before RESTRICTEDNEVER clips it. It is a filter-state difference, not an engine difference. The fix is open item 3 in tonight’s EOD; the certification method is in there too.
