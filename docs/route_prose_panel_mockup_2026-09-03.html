What this is

Fred, 09-03: "it was frustrating to demo and have to navigate three screens to show route info. One click from the map would have been awesome."

⭐ A layer in front of the artifact panel, not a change to it. onRouteTap opens this; Details opens the artifact panel exactly as it is today. Nothing existing changes shape.

⚠ And an old route with no narrative goes straight to the artifact panel (Fred). The prose panel never renders empty — which is the failure that would make it feel broken.

✅ Already stored — reads, not new work
  • Miles and the hours range — on the route row. ⭐ The RANGE, not the low end: a 3-to-4.5 hour ride reading as 3 sends a rider out expecting to be home before dark.
  • The prose — written at build time into route_notes, already exported as GPX <desc>.
  • Trails with mileage — the compare table's data, so the trails carrying the most miles come first.
  • Surface per trail — the two-query lookup shipped today works here unchanged.
⚠ Points along the way — the one open question

There is no POI concept on a route. Three candidates and they are not the same thing:

  • Waypoints near the line — ⭐ the only source carrying a rider's own knowledge ("gate may be closed"). Needs a corridor query, and distance-along-route is derived rather than stored.
  • The recipe's pins — already there, but they are INPUTS. Nobody marked them as interesting.
  • Features the narrative names — free, but prose, so there is no distance to put beside them.

⚠ The twisty is CLOSED by default, so this can ship after the rest.

⛔ Two defects underneath this
  • The ride map has NO route tap at all. convoy_map.html has no onRouteTap wiring — routes fall through to the Leaflet default there. The planner has it.
  • Trails now win taps over routes. Trails got a handler this morning and draw ON TOP, so they swallow the click. ⭐ Layer order should be trails, then tracks, then routes — rider-owned above source data. ⚠ And it must be reasserted after every trail redraw, because a pan rebuilds that layer.

⭐ Both are prerequisites. A prose panel behind a tap that does not fire is worth nothing.

Size

400dp wide, about 330 tall with the trails twisty open. Comfortable on a tablet, which is what riders use.
