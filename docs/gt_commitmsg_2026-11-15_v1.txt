FOUR RULES EARNED TODAY:
  1. Verify by DECLARED NAME, never by filename. ConvoyDownloadQueuePanel.kt declares
     DownloadQueuePanel; a filename grep reported 0 refs for a composable with 4 live callers.
     This morning's 16 route removals were safe precisely because the tool matched declarations.
  2. A reference means nothing without its GUARD. A call site inside a dead branch is a
     reference to nothing.
  3. A count means nothing without a NAMED CALLER, and one hop to a root. "Someone calls it"
     is the aggregate claim that failed all day.
  4. Every run carries a BUDGET. Osm15g's 733 s against a 737 s expectation was trustworthy at
     once; Import15e's 20 minutes against 187 s was unambiguously wrong. Four stalls today came
     from optimistic estimates against a 180 s cap -- the rule binds the estimator too.
