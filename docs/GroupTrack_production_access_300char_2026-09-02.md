## 1) How did you recruit users for your closed test?
> **[262 chars]**

Recruited directly from the off-road riding community — side-by-side, UTV, ATV,
dirt bike and Jeep riders, exactly who the app is built for. Around 30 came that
way from March 2026, plus 15 from a paid testing service for device coverage I
could not reach alone.

---

## 2) How easy was it to recruit testers for your app?

**Easy**

⚠ Not a text field — a choice. "Easy" is honest given 30 riders volunteered.

---

## 3) Describe the engagement you received from testers during your closed test
> **[276 chars]**

Testers used it on real rides, not to satisfy a counter. One rider who uses Ride
Command, Avenza, Gaia and onX told me he opened GroupTrack three times and could
not work out how to use it. That single message reshaped the release — navigation
and route planning were rebuilt.

---

## 4) Provide a summary of the feedback you received. Include how you collected it.
> **[272 chars]**

Collected by email, message, phone and crash reports. Themes: route planning too
complex; the planner ignored dirt roads riders knew were rideable; repeat searches
in one area got worse; icons were unreadable; downloaded maps survived uninstall
with no way to remove them.

---

## 5) Who is the intended audience for your app?
> **[239 chars]**

Off-road riders — side-by-side, UTV, ATV, dirt bike and Jeep — who ride trails
beyond cell coverage, usually in groups. They lose contact with each other and
with their maps exactly where it matters most. GroupTrack works entirely offline.

---

## 6) Describe how your app provides value to the users.
> **[276 chars]**

Three things a rider cannot otherwise do off-grid: navigate with no service, since
maps and trail data are on the device; keep a group together, as riders see each
other over mesh radio rather than cell; and plan a ride the app builds from the
trail network to their distance.

---

## 7) How many installs do you expect in your first year?

**1k – 10k**

> ⭐ If a comment field is offered, **[249]**:

Around 1,000 riders, deliberately. Production access is about distribution
friction, not scale — today every tester must be vetted and pre-registered by
email before installing, which is awkward for them and does not scale for me.

---

## 8) What changes did you make based on what you learned during your closed test?
> **[270 chars]** — ⭐ **the question that carries the application**

Rebuilt route planning to one prompt at a time after a rider said he could not use
it. Fixed repeat searches returning worse results. Added map deletion by area — the
testers' design, not mine. Replaced icons with text labels. Excluded a device class
that cannot run it.

---

## 9) How did you decide your app is ready for production?
> **[261 chars]**

Every feature a rider needs on a trail is complete and has been used on real rides.
Faults testers found are fixed and verified on device, not just in code. It runs
for hours with the screen on, recording, with no network — the condition it is
actually used in.

---

## 10) What did you do differently this time?
> **[263 chars]**

First application. Before opening a Play closed test I ran six months with ~30
experienced riders, distributing builds directly. Several have search-and-rescue
and Meshtastic backgrounds, and all use the commercial alternatives, so their
comparisons are informed.
