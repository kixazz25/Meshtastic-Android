4What CLEAR TILES actually does BUILT

1  begin()            record written — ABORT if it cannot be written
2  holdQueue()        no new jobs start
3  cancelAll()        cancels DOWNLOADING workers (keeps COMPLETE)
4  awaitIdle()        poll ≤15s — cancelWorkById is ASYNC and returns early.
                      timeout ⇒ FAIL, do not delete under a live worker
5  clearCompleted()   drains the history cancelAll deliberately kept
6  deleteSource(dir)  per OLD-source cache dir, noteDeleted() after each
7  complete()         + resumeQueue()

⭐ Step 4 is the one that earns its place. deleteSource closes the cached handle; doing that under a running worker risks a half-written store.

⭐ Resume is free. Already-deleted dirs are skipped, so running clearColumn on an interrupted record finishes the job rather than repeating it.

⚠ resumeQueue() on every exit path including failure — a failed migration that also leaves the queue paused is a worse bug than the one being avoided.

⭐⭐ This is the answer to "where did my tiles go?"

The migration deliberately destroys the queue history — the one record that would otherwise say what had been downloaded. So the record lives in maps/source_changes/, a sibling of maps/tiles/, deliberately outside everything a clear touches.

⚠ Interrupted records prompt, never auto-resume. Finishing means deleting GB of tiles, and the launch that discovers it may be at a trailhead.

Decisions this flow encodes

DecisionWhy
Refresh is not a menu itemThe transition only makes sense at the moment the source changes — that is when "Esri → Google Hybrid" is a known fact rather than something the app must infer.
Clear and reload are separateSeparates the action from interrelated decisions; you pick based on your needs, nothing is automated.
Existing users are never movedwriteColumnFile:253 — the saved column wins over the asset default. Google Hybrid is a new-install default only.
Clear ⇒ replace = falseOn an empty store there is nothing to replace, and hasTile then deduplicates across the whole batch — overlapping corridors download shared tiles once.
Clear is its own job at priority 0Queue order is not guaranteed at MAX_CONCURRENT = 2; a per-entry flag means whichever job runs first wipes what the others wrote.
Beta recommendation: clear and start over15 testers, one path, one configuration to reason about. ⚠ The strength of the recommendation is text that softens later — the mechanism does not change.

Open before this is finished

  • KEEP TILES must complete its record — otherwise it dangles as a permanently interrupted migration and pollutes the next source change on that column.
  • Segmentation threshold — 60,000 or 50,000? One number is stale, and the reload estimate rests on it.
  • Startup prompt for unfinished migrations — hasUnfinished() exists; nothing calls it yet.
  • Union of old and new cache dirs — the clear deletes the OLD source's stores. If the NEW source's store already exists with stale tiles, it survives. Deliberate for now (a shared cache dir could belong to another column) but worth a decision.
  • Prove on Droid 2 before Droid 1. This deletes GB with no backup.
