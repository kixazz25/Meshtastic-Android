GroupTrack website: tagging, articles and site improvement

Living doc. Version 2, 29 October 2026 EOD (v1 08:01–08:25; v2 adds sections 8–10 and the 12:21–14:56 changes) (Fred 08:01–08:25). Edit in place; it holds the rules, not references to them.

The site's one job: take an average rider from “what is this?” to riding with GroupTrack, and give riders who have it the documents.
Audience: the average rider, 55–75, wary of technology. Not early adopters. Keep it light, plain words, short sentences.
The two messages, in order: 1. download it and take it for a test drive; 2. if you're not impressed, uninstall it (no account to close, nothing to cancel).
Voice: Fred, first person. Nine months building it; on a mission to build not just a tool but something that brings riders together and supports the riding community. Every article asks: find something missing, awkward or broken? Reach out.

6. The publisher

  • Queue (outside the web root): /var/www/articles_queue/ holds articles.json, pages/NN_slug.html, publish_articles.py and publish.log.
  • Runs every morning at 10:07 server time (UTC) = 6:07 am Eastern (5:07 in winter): grouptrack-articles.timer → grouptrack-articles.service. A missed morning runs at the next boot.
  • An article goes live when its date (US Eastern) has come. Running it twice the same day changes nothing.
  • It writes only: articles/, sitemap.xml, the Sitemap line in robots.txt, and the block between <!-- ARTICLES-START --> and <!-- ARTICLES-END --> in index.html (plus showing each step's “Read the article” link once that article is live).
  • Any day: bash "$GT_INBOX/site_articles_2026-10-29/site_articles_2026-10-29.sh" status shows the last runs and the next one.
  • Changing an article or a date: edit it in the package source on Drive, rebuild, and ship it as a new dated package with its own install script and backup (never edit the server copy by hand).
  • Adding article 13 onward: same: a new dated package adds the page and a line in articles.json.
  • Undo: the package's undo stops the timer, restores index.html, go.php and robots/sitemap from the backup, and moves articles/, gt14.css and the queue into the backup folder (moves, not deletes).

9. Facebook posting plan (Fred 10:20–14:32)
