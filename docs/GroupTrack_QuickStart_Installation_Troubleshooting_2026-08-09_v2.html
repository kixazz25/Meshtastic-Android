GroupTrack — Quick Start, Installation & Troubleshooting
Beta Tester Reference · Updated August 9, 2026 (V2.6e)

Beta Tester Reference

⛔ DO NOT CLEAR APP DATA OR CACHE

Android Settings → Apps → GroupTrack → Storage → Clear storage / Clear cache will cost you your trails, tracks and routes.

Clearing app data makes GroupTrack lose its permission to reach its own files, while Android still shows the permission as granted. The next time the app opens it cannot see your data, treats the device as new, and rebuilds empty — over the top of everything you had.

If you have already cleared app data or cache: DO NOT OPEN GROUPTRACK. Uninstall it first, then install it again. Your maps and trails live in Documents/GroupTrack and survive an uninstall — they do not survive the app opening without permission.

Do not copy your files anywhere and do not move them back by hand. A fresh install finds them on its own. Copying them back into an app that cannot see them is how they get lost.

Clearing app data is never a fix for a GroupTrack problem. If something is wrong, uninstall and reinstall instead — it is safe, and it keeps your data.

Changing your satellite map source

Screens for this are shown step by step in the User Manual — tap the ? button in the app.

When you change the map source on the SAT slot, GroupTrack asks what to do with the map tiles you have already downloaded. Your maps keep working the whole time — this is only about tiles stored on your device. Online maps switch to the new source straight away.

You get three choices:

  • Delete and start over. Clears the stored tiles for that slot and lets you rebuild from scratch. Best if you have only a little downloaded, or you would rather not think about it — it cannot leave you half converted.
  • Move everything to the new source. Tick what you want moved: track maps, map areas, or both. At least one must be ticked. Everything is submitted at once and runs in the background.
  • Leave my maps in place. Nothing is downloaded or removed. Refresh ground yourself from the downloads panel when you need it. You can also tick Add track maps now to bring your trails over immediately and leave the areas for later.

If you have tracks, tick "Refresh track corridors". Leaving it off does not lose your tracks — but the same ground gets covered as boxed areas instead, which downloads roughly ten times as many tiles for it.

While the move runs, a panel lists each step as it finishes. Removing track maps is the slow part and shows a progress bar. Leave the panel on screen until it is done — it cannot be restarted from where it stopped.

Watching downloads

Screens for this are shown step by step in the User Manual — tap the ? button in the app.

Tap QUEUES to see what is downloading and what is waiting. The panel shows:

  • What is running and what is waiting — the number of jobs and the tile count for each, with the estimated time remaining and the date and time it expects to finish.
  • Live speed — the combined tiles per second across everything currently downloading. This is the total, not one job’s share.
  • Slots — how many downloads run at once, 1 to 4. 4 is recommended and is the default on a new install. Your setting is remembered. Lowering it does not stop jobs already running; it takes effect as they finish.
  • Filter chips — ALL, AREA, CORR, RFSH, DEL — narrow the list to one kind of job. They also scope the buttons: with a filter active, CANCEL and CLEAR act only on what you can see.
  • QUEUED and COMPLETED — tap the arrow beside either heading to collapse a long list and move quickly to the section you want. Tap a completed job to see when it started, how long it took, and its average speed.

Downloads run faster across different map sources than within one. Each source is a separate server, so jobs spread over satellite, topo and topo+ finish sooner than the same number of jobs all pulling from one source. A refresh that touches only one slot will take longer than a mixed download of the same size — that is normal, not a fault.

For a long download, plug in and take the app off battery saving. A phone left on battery overnight can run at a fraction of its daytime speed. Use a wall charger rather than a computer — a sleeping computer can cut power to its USB ports. Then, in Android Settings, find GroupTrack under Battery and set it to Unrestricted, and turn off any standby or app optimisation settings for it. The exact wording varies by phone.

If the queue is holding, the panel says QUEUE HELD — nothing will start and offers RELEASE. A held queue looks exactly like a stalled one, so check for that banner first if nothing seems to be happening.

QUICK START

⚠️ Off-grid rule: trails have no cell/WiFi. Download your maps and import your trails BEFORE you ride — the app can't fetch them in the field. Un-downloaded areas show blank on the trail.

Before Your First Ride

  1. Install GroupTrack APK on your Android device (see Installation below)
  2. Power on your T1000-E Meshtastic radio
  3. Open GroupTrack — radio should connect via Bluetooth automatically
  4. Verify connection: radio icon shows connected in the app

⚠️ CRITICAL — Two Radio Restrictions

RESTRICTION 1 — T1000-E Firmware: DO NOT update beyond 2.6.11

The T1000-E Meshtastic radio firmware MUST stay at version 2.6.11 or earlier. Updating to any firmware version beyond 2.6.11 will make the radio unstable for Bluetooth connectivity and radio configuration. This is a known issue with newer Meshtastic firmware on the T1000-E hardware.

  • Check current firmware: Meshtastic app → Radio Config → Device Info
  • If firmware is 2.6.11 or earlier: DO NOT UPDATE
  • If firmware was accidentally updated beyond 2.6.11: flash back to 2.6.11 using Meshtastic Flasher tool
  • This restriction applies to ALL T1000-E radios used with GroupTrack

RESTRICTION 2 — Keep Radio Within Bluetooth Range During Rides

When riding, ALWAYS keep your radio in your cart/vehicle within 50 feet of your phone. Do NOT carry the radio away from your phone (or vice versa) beyond Bluetooth effective range.

  • Bluetooth effective range: approximately 50 feet (15 meters)
  • If radio exceeds BLE range while still "connected," the connection enters supervision timeout
  • Symptoms: app freezes/pauses, position updates stop, UI becomes unresponsive
  • The BLE connection does not cleanly disconnect — it hangs in limbo waiting for the radio to reappear
  • Recovery: force close GroupTrack, move radio back within range, reopen app
  • Prevention: leave the radio mounted in your cart. Keep your phone in the cart. They travel together.

Pre-Ride Checklist

  • [ ] Phone charged
  • [ ] Radio charged and firmware at 2.6.11 or earlier
  • [ ] Radio mounted in cart (stays with phone during ride)
  • [ ] GroupTrack installed and opens without errors
  • [ ] Radio connects via Bluetooth (solid connection icon)
  • [ ] Map tiles downloaded for the ENTIRE ride area (REQUIRED off-grid — check the blue overlay covers it on Planning Map)
  • [ ] Trail data / trailheads imported for ride area (also needed before you lose connectivity)
  • [ ] GPS lock acquired (position shows on map)

INSTALLATION

⚠️ Install with -r only — never -d
The correct command is adb install -r <apk>. The -d flag allows a downgrade, and a downgrade makes Android treat the install as brand new. The app then starts without all-files access and can overwrite its own map databases with empty ones. Without -d, a bad version number simply makes the install fail with an error — which is what you want.
⚠️ If the install asks first-time setup questions on a phone that already has GroupTrack — CANCEL
That means Android is treating it as a fresh install rather than an update, and continuing can cost your downloaded maps and trail data. Cancel, and check the version number before trying again.
⚠️ Sideloaded and Play Store builds cannot replace each other
Both are named com.grouptrack.android, but they are signed with different keys. A sideloaded build blocks the Play Store from updating the app, and vice versa. Switching between them requires an uninstall first — which erases your downloaded maps. Pick one and stay with it.
✅ Ten-second check after any install
Open the app and confirm your trails and tracks are still listed. If everything looks empty, stop and report it before downloading anything — do not re-download over the top.

Installing GroupTrack APK

From Play Store (tester):

  • Accept tester invitation link
  • Download from Play Store
  • Package: com.grouptrack.android

From local APK (developer/tester):

adb -s [device-serial] install -r app/build/outputs/apk/google/release/app-google-release.apk

⚠️ INSTALL RULE (CORRECTED 2026-08-02): use plain -r. NEVER add -d. The previous guidance here said to always use -r -d; that was wrong and it cost a full spatial database on 2026-08-01. -d permits a DOWNGRADE, a downgrade makes Android treat the install as brand new, the app then starts without all-files authority, and its create-if-missing database open writes empty schemas over the real ones. Without -d a regressed version code makes the install FAIL with an error instead of silently downgrading — the failure is the warning you want. Add -d only after establishing why the version code went backwards.

⚠️ If an install prompts FIRST-INSTALL questions on a device that already has GroupTrack: CANCEL. That is the signature of the above. Do not proceed.

✅ Ten-second post-install check: adb -s [device-serial] shell ls -la /sdcard/Documents/GroupTrack/*.db — a spatial database in the hundreds of KB rather than tens of MB means it has been replaced with an empty schema. Stop and restore rather than re-downloading over the top.

⚠️ Play Store vs local APK: DIFFERENT SIGNING KEYS, SAME PACKAGE NAME. Since the 07-26 package merge both are com.grouptrack.android, so a sideloaded build blocks the Play Store from installing or updating the app and vice versa (INSTALL_FAILED_UPDATE_INCOMPATIBLE, or Play silently offers nothing). To move a device between the two you must UNINSTALL first — which wipes tiles and app data. Decide per device: sideload OR Play, not both.

Post-Install Steps

  1. Launch GroupTrack
  2. Grant location permission when prompted
  3. Open Planning Map and verify tile loading

First-Time Setup (do these in order)

Before your first ride, complete these three setup steps. For detailed, screen-by-screen walkthroughs with screenshots, open the in-app manual — tap the "?" icon anywhere in GroupTrack. The manual is cookbook-style (how you get there → what you do → where it leads); the steps below are the short version.

1. Settings → choose your map sources. Open Settings and select the map sources you want available. New installations use Google Hybrid for the satellite slot (SAT), with Esri Topographic (TOPO) and Esri USA Topo (TOPO+). Leave the defaults unless you have a reason to change them — these are what the download and map screens use. (Manual: "?" → Settings / Map Sources.)

⚠️ Upgrading testers: your satellite slot does NOT change. If you already have GroupTrack installed you keep whatever source you were using — the new default applies to fresh installations only, and nothing is downloaded or deleted behind your back. Google Hybrid is worth moving to: it carries roads and place names inside the satellite imagery, so it needs one tile store instead of three (Esri satellite needs two extra label stores) and shows street names offline. To switch, go to Settings → Map Sources → SAT and choose Google Hybrid; GroupTrack then walks you through what to do with the tiles you already stored. See Changing a map source below.

Changing a map source (SAT, TOPO or TOPO+)

Stored tiles came from the source that downloaded them and cannot be converted. When you change the source on a slot that already holds tiles, GroupTrack asks what to do with them.

⚠️ Please be advised: whichever option you choose, you lose Esri place and road label information. The new source does not fetch label overlays, so those label stores are removed either way — and their space (typically a few hundred MB) is reclaimed.

You will be asked to choose one of three:

  • Delete and start over — removes the stored maps for that slot and frees the space, so you can rebuild around your tracks. This cannot be undone and there is no backup. Your download history for that slot is cleared too, so note any areas you will want to redraw. Recommended when moving from Esri to Google Hybrid, unless you are already downloading by corridor.
  • Move everything to the new source — GroupTrack converts your existing maps in the background. All of the downloads are submitted at once and every image is slowly replaced from the new source, track maps first and then your areas. Your maps stay usable throughout. Best if you rely on road and place names. (Arriving shortly — not in this build.)
  • Move tracks and areas on my schedule — nothing is removed and nothing is downloaded. Your existing maps keep working, so you always have something to fall back on. Refresh ground as you need it from the downloads panel — by track, or by drawing an area — with Replace tiles ticked. Ground you have not refreshed stays without road and place names.

Nothing downloads automatically. When you are ready, use Download Tiles by Area or by Track. If you have imported tracks, downloading by corridor around those tracks is the better choice — it covers where you actually ride and uses roughly two thirds less storage than boxing the same ground by area. If you have no tracks yet, download by area and add corridors later; the two work together in the same store.

Your tracks, routes and trails are never affected — this only ever touches downloaded map tiles.

2. Import trails and trailheads, and DOWNLOAD MAPS for your ride area. Load the trail data for your riding area so trails and trailheads show on the map. Then, while on WiFi/cell, download the map tiles for the area you'll ride — use the Planning Map download and confirm the blue overlay covers your whole ride area. (Manual: "?" → Import Artifacts / Trail Sources, and Offline Maps & Downloads.) If trails don't appear, see "Trail Display Issues" in Troubleshooting.

2b. Import OSM trail data (optional, and worth doing). GroupTrack can import free OpenStreetMap data for your state, which typically adds far more ground than the curated trail sources alone — for Utah, about 104,000 trails and unpaved roads against roughly 49,000 from the state source. It is a four-step process on one panel: Planning Map → Work with Artifacts → Import OSM Data.

  1. DOWNLOAD — the panel opens the Geofabrik site in your browser. Choose your state and take the file ending in -latest-free.gpkg.zip (about 330 MB for Utah). ⚠️ The other formats on that page will download and then be rejected.
  2. CLICK HERE WHEN DOWNLOAD HAS COMPLETED — nothing happens automatically; GroupTrack does not watch your downloads folder. Tap the green bar and it finds the file.
  3. EXTRACT — unpacks and builds a trail database. Under a minute for most states.
  4. IMPORT — choose FULL STATE, or SELECTED AREA to draw a box on the map and take only what is inside it. The confirmation tells you how many trails are about to be added. There is no undo.
  5. CLEANUP — removes the working files. Not automatic; it waits for you.

Importing a state you already have is safe: it reports almost everything as dropped and adds nothing, so you will not get two copies of a trail.

(Manual: "?" → Planning Map → Work with Artifacts → Import OSM Data — an eight-screen walkthrough with captures of both the whole-state and drawn-area paths.)

⚠️ Downloading maps before you ride is REQUIRED for off-grid use. On the trail you are off the grid — no cell, no WiFi — so the app cannot fetch map tiles in the field. Any area you did not download ahead of time will show blank/grey on the trail. Download every area you plan to ride before you leave connectivity, and verify the blue overlay covers it. This applies to trails/trailheads too — import them ahead of time.

3. Set up the radio. Pair and connect the T1000-E (see Radio Setup below). Do this last, after maps and trails are in place. (Manual: "?" → Radio / Connection.)

Note for upgrading testers: if you're coming from a pre-V2.6 build, do the V2.6 One-Time Tile Migration (below) before step 2's tile downloads.

⚠️ V2.6 One-Time Tile Migration (upgrading testers)

V2.6 changes how offline map tiles are stored — from thousands of loose files to a single compact database per map type (about half the storage; satellite ~55% smaller, WebP-compressed). Old loose tiles must be cleared once and re-downloaded in the new format before maps will work.

  1. Install X-plore File Manager (free, Play Store) — it shows a live progress count while deleting, so you always know it's working.
  2. In X-plore, go to Internal storage → Documents → GroupTrack → maps and delete the maps folder. A large old cache can take a while — keep the device plugged in; the on-screen count shows it's active.
  3. Back in GroupTrack, re-download your ride areas (by Area, or from a track/route). They save in the new format at roughly half the size.

Your tracks and routes are NOT affected — this clears only downloaded map tiles. This is one-time; future updates won't need it.

Radio Setup

  1. Power on T1000-E radio
  2. Verify firmware is 2.6.11 or earlier — DO NOT UPDATE
  3. Program the radio to the master config default. In GroupTrack, go to Convoy Map → Event Ride menu → Update Radio Config. This loads the T1000-E with the GroupTrack master configuration (the standard settings all convoy radios share) so your radio interoperates with the group. Do this before relying on the radio for a ride — a radio on different settings won't work with the convoy. (Manual: "?" → Radio / Connection.)
  4. Open GroupTrack → radio should appear in device list
  5. Select radio to pair
  6. Verify connection: position icon, signal strength visible

Device Compatibility

Supported: Android 14 to 16 devices with a Bluetooth radio and a GPS chipset. Both are required — Bluetooth to hold the connection to the T1000-E radio, and an onboard GPS chipset for position. A device missing either (e.g. a Wi-Fi-only tablet with no GPS) is not compatible.

| Requirement | Needed | |-------------|--------| | Android version | 14, 15, or 16 | | Bluetooth | Required (BLE, to connect the T1000-E) | | GPS chipset | Required (onboard location) |

Known-incompatible pattern: budget tablets that lack a proper BLE radio (or a GPS chipset) cannot hold the connection to the T1000-E — symptom is a repeating connect/disconnect every 8–10 seconds (supervision timeout reason 0x0008). This is a hardware limitation of those devices, not an app bug; use a device meeting the requirements above.

TROUBLESHOOTING

BLE Connection Issues

Symptom: App freezes or pauses during ride

  • Cause: Radio moved beyond Bluetooth range (~50') while still connected. BLE supervision timeout.
  • Fix: Force close GroupTrack. Move radio back within range of phone. Reopen app.
  • Prevention: Keep radio mounted in cart with phone. They must stay within 50' of each other at all times during a ride.

Symptom: Radio connects then disconnects repeatedly (8-10 second cycle)

  • Cause: Device hardware incompatibility (Tab8NEU, budget Android tablets). Supervision timeout reason 0x0008.
  • Fix: Use a compatible device (see Device Compatibility table above). This is a hardware issue, not an app bug.

Symptom: Radio won't connect at all after firmware update

  • Cause: T1000-E firmware updated beyond 2.6.11.
  • Fix: Flash radio back to firmware 2.6.11 using Meshtastic Flasher tool. Do NOT use any firmware newer than 2.6.11.

Symptom: Radio connects but position doesn't update

  • Cause: Radio may be out of GPS fix, or BLE connection is degraded.
  • Fix: Check radio has clear sky view for GPS. Force close and reconnect. Verify radio firmware is 2.6.11.

Map and Tile Issues

Symptom: Map shows blank/grey tiles

  • Cause: No tile cache for this area. Online tiles failed to load (no connectivity or tile source down).
  • Fix: Check connectivity. Download tiles for this area while on WiFi. Check blue overlay on Planning Map to confirm tile coverage.

Symptom: Double labels on satellite map

  • Cause: Old cached tiles (from a hybrid URL) showing baked-in labels plus new overlay labels — typically pre-V2.6 tiles not yet migrated.
  • Fix: Do the V2.6 one-time tile migration (Installation → V2.6 One-Time Tile Migration): delete the maps folder via X-plore and re-download. New-format tiles carry labels as a separate overlay, so the doubling goes away.

Symptom: Map tiles load online but show blank offline

  • Cause: Tiles not downloaded for this area.
  • Fix: Before going to the field, download tiles for your ride area using Planning Map download function. Verify blue overlay covers your ride area.

Trail Display Issues

Symptom: No trails showing on map

  • Cause: No trail source downloaded, or trail database not loaded.
  • Fix: Open Planning Map → Work with Artifacts → select and download a source (e.g., UGRC Utah Trails).

Symptom: Trails showing in wrong location

  • Cause: Coordinate system mismatch or corrupt GeoJSON source.
  • Fix: Remove trail source and re-download/re-ingest.

App Issues

Symptom: App is slow or unresponsive on launch

  • Fix: Force close and reopen. If it keeps happening, report it to the GroupTrack team with your device model and what you were doing.

Symptom: Missing data after an update

  • Good news: Your saved content is stored outside the app and survives updates and reinstalls — recorded/imported tracks and routes (in Documents/GroupTrack/data/) and your downloaded map tiles (in Documents/GroupTrack/maps/) are kept.
  • If maps look empty after a major update: you may need the one-time tile migration — see "V2.6 One-Time Tile Migration" in Installation.

Emergency Recovery

⛔ Never use Clear storage or Clear cache. See the warning at the top of this document — it will cost you your trails, tracks and routes. Uninstall and reinstall instead; that is safe.

If everything is broken:

  1. Uninstall GroupTrack
  2. Flash the T1000-E to firmware 2.6.11
  3. Reinstall the APK with adb install -r (plain -r — do not add -d)
  4. Launch — spatial data in Documents/GroupTrack/ should still be there
  5. Reconnect the radio
  6. Re-download tiles if the cache was lost

⚠️ Your trails, tracks, routes and waypoints live in Documents/GroupTrack/ and survive an uninstall. Downloaded map tiles do not.
