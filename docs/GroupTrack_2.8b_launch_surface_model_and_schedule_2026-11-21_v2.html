4 · WHAT CERTIFIES A SURFACE

Stage one, the day's work: the seam is repaired from existing services and both targets compile plus the iOS build runs. That is where the day ends.

Stage two, separately (C28-26374 UNITTEST3WAY): the three-way side-by-side — 2.7f on Droid 2 · 2.8b on Droid 1 · 2.8b on the iPad — from ONE database, walking the menus A to J, the same order as the build. 1↔2 proves the service move changed nothing on Android; 2↔3 proves the platforms run the same process.

⚠ THE RADIO EXCEPTION, from the code: GtIosCommConfig takes clientOf: () -> RadioClient? and its header says that is “always null on the simulator, which has no Bluetooth”. So every radio function reads NOT_CONNECTED on the sim whatever is built. Radio surfaces certify 1↔2 on the droids; 2↔3 waits for the sideloaded iPad. A NotSupported on the sim is not a port gap.
