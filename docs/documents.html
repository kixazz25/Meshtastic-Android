The App in Action

Off-Grid Group Tracking — Live on the Trail

GroupTrack running at Gooseberry Mesa — a 6.4 mile ride with real-time lead tracking over LoRa mesh radio. No cell service. No WiFi. Every rider on the map.

The hybrid satellite map, offline tile cache, and lead telemetry panel are all visible here — exactly what your group sees on the trail.

  • Hybrid satellite map with offline tile cache
  • Live lead telemetry — speed, heading, altitude, battery
  • LoRa mesh radio — no cell or WiFi required
  • Lead track drawn in real time as the group moves
  • GroupTrack nav panel — always accessible from the sidebar
