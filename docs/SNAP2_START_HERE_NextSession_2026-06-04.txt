----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 — FIX, by what Step 1 showed
----------------------------------------------------------------
2A. trails=0 (starving) — MOST LIKELY
    - Check the bounds in the log: are S/W/N/E sane for where you are, or are
      they 0.0 / stale? ConvoyScreen seeds lastViewport* = 0.0 and sets them on
      the first onViewportChanged. If you entered route mode and tapped before a
      viewport event, bounds are 0 -> query returns nothing.
      FIX: ensure bounds are current at tap — either read map.getBounds() via JS
      at tap time, or guarantee onViewportChanged has fired (it normally has).
    - Check argument ORDER: queryTrailsByViewport(south, west, north, east).
      Confirm the call passes them in that order (a known prior gotcha: the
      query signature vs call-site order — verify against where_used).
    - Check DB init + that trails actually exist in the spatial DB for this area
      (Work-with-Artifacts -> Trails ON shows them? if trails don't DISPLAY,
      they won't snap either).
    - Confirm zoom: below the trail display min, the set may be empty.

2B. trails>0, snapResult=null (math/radius)
    - Radius: 30 m may be too tight for a finger tap. Temporarily raise to 60-80
      m to confirm snapping works at all, then tune down.
    - Geometry parse: snap() uses parseWktLine -> List<DoubleArray>. Confirm the
      DoubleArray is [lon,lat] vs [lat,lon] consistently with how snap measures
      distance. A lon/lat swap makes every distance huge -> nothing in radius.
    - Confirm the "geometry" map key holds WKT LINESTRING text snap can parse.

2C. snapResult good but point draws at tap (not used)
    - Ensure the vertex ADDED is snapToVertex(s), not freeVertex, and that
      drawBuildLine uses routeVertices() AFTER addVertex. Re-read the onMapTap
      block; the snapped lat/lon must be what's pushed to JS.

2D. SnapDiag never logs (tap not reaching snap)
    - onMapTap JS bridge not firing in route mode, or armed-state gating it.
      Confirm setRouteMode(true) ran and convoy_map.html emits Android.onMapTap.
      (This overlaps the future armed-gating work.)
