IMPORT TRAILS

Trail data has been assembled from a trusted group of public authorities. Some groups are regional or state-specific. Others are nationwide.

We have crafted two import methods for you to identify the trails you may need.

By source — Import by the source owner: Utah, USFS, BLM, and others.

By area — Draw a bounding box around your riding areas and select all trail info in these areas. GroupTrack will show you which sources cover trails in those areas and you can select as many resources as you like.

Browse the available sources to get comfortable with what they are and what they represent. Develop a strategy of which sources you want in their entirety and which areas you want to focus on.

Do your full sources first — they take longer. We will keep you informed of how many trails are being inspected and how many are being imported, with a recap for each source.

You can combine these methods — full source for local resources like your home state, and area selection for nationwide resources like BLM and USFS to get just the trails in your riding corridor.
