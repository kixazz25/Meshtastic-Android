Our Mission

Convoy Coordination.
Without the Network.

GroupTrack keeps your group connected when cell service disappears. Built on Meshtastic’s low-power LoRa mesh radio technology, every rider in your convoy can see every other rider’s position in real time — no towers, no data plan, no excuses.

Whether you’re leading a UTV convoy through the canyon country of southern Utah, coordinating a group trail ride in the backcountry, or managing search and rescue operations in a dead zone — GroupTrack gives your group the situational awareness it needs to stay safe, stay together, and stay on track.


Pre-download your maps. Configure your radios. Head out. GroupTrack does the rest.

What It Does

Built for the Trail.

Every feature in GroupTrack was designed for real-world off-grid use — by riders, for riders.

📍
Live Convoy Map
See your entire group on an interactive map in real time. Color-coded by rider position in the convoy. Never lose track of who’s ahead or behind.
🌐
Offline Map Downloads
Search for your trail area, draw your download zone, and cache map tiles before you leave. Satellite, topo, hybrid, and road layers all work offline.
🔋
Mesh Radio Network
Powered by Meshtastic LoRa radio. Each device acts as a relay, extending range across the entire convoy automatically.
📄
Ride Management
Organizers create rides, set radio configs, and invite riders with a single link. Riders tap the link, download the ride, and go. Zero technical setup required.
🏭
GPS Track Recording
Record your ride track with one tap. Pause, resume, and save. Export as KML for Google Earth or share with the group after the ride.
⚡
One-Boot Radio Config
Set channel, frequency, PSK, and device settings from the app. Apply with one tap. Radio reboots configured and ready to mesh in under 60 seconds.
